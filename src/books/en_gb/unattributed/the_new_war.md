# 14th June, 2XXX

  So... we're fighting them, they're fighting us. Neither of us refuses to cede even an inch. Our greatest military minds hatch a plan: they give up. Not entirely, mind you: they set up a DMZ. A huge wall and five unbroken miles of land so treacherous neither us nor them could get through it without suffering major casualties. And they sat their <filter as="bums">arses</filter> down and waited. The idea was that eventually the aliens would get so complacent that after a couple of generations future humanity will be able to spin its war efforts back up and wipe them out.

  Couple of generations passed, about a hundred years. Humanity has, by and large, not even seen an alien in that time, but we *have* been lying in wait, training for the moment that we strike back. Our AIs and databanks keeping the strategic knowledge alive. Right?

  *Eehrt*, WRONG! Turns out the aliens we future humans left our walls to fight are the same <filter as="flipping">bloody</filter> aliens our forebears fought, down to the individual! And they haven't lost a step. Whatever they endured on the long trip here, combined with three hundred years of fighting us? A million times more effective a teacher than sims and drills could ever be. Our greatest military minds put their heads together and not a one thought "Wait, what if these things don't age like us?" and now WE'RE the complacent ones losing steps in a war that used to be a stalemate.

  No ifs, ands, or buts about it, we are <filter as="Stuffed">Buggered</filter>. <filter as="Capital-S Stuffed">Capital-B Buggered</filter>. They haven't invented a state of being <filter as="Stuffed">Buggered</filter> that's <filter as="Stuffed">Buggered</filter> as much as we are <filter as="Stuffed">Buggered</filter> right now.

  Well, I've had enough. I'm not wasting my life fighting these <filter as="flipping">bloody</filter> immortal spiders any longer! Next cargo ship home I'm taking it, I swear!
