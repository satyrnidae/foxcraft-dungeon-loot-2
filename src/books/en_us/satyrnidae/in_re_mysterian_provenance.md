Dr. Melisova:

It pains me a great deal to hear of the destruction of a valuable archaeological site. In the case you ever find another, be sure to send me a notification as soon as possible so I and my colleagues can plan an expedition with haste.

Unfortunately due to its removal from its previous resting place I cannot confirm *Palomina and the Daemon Lord*'s provenance any more than you could in the field. Context really is everything; was this a story that was told among the people of the lost civilization? Is it a *post hoc* invention? I cannot be sure.

I have been granted leave by the Ethics Committee to commit the book to my personal collection in lieu of proceeding with further study, until the eventuality of my death, at which point it will be interred among the many other books of questionable validity in the University's private library.

At the very least, I commend your effort to see that the book reached an expert on the Mysterians such as myself. If you find anything else, do feel free to send it my way... *if* it has already left its previous context, that is.

Yours truly,

<style class=".signature">Doctor F Braun</style>
Director of Archaeology, New Tyne Academy
