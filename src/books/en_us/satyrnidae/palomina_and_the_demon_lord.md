# - I -


<span class="drop">I</span>n Time Immemorial, the God of the Sun <span class=".name">Mysteria</span> fought the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span> <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> and sealed It away, deep below the Earth.

For a long time, Humanity prospered under <span class=".name">Mysteria</span>'s Light, building great civilizations and filling many libraries with the tales of their exploits.

But in their endless search for knowledge, they discovered the prison of the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span>, and loosed It upon the unsuspecting Earth once more.

Wasting no time, <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> struck out against <span class=".name">Mysteria</span> and, catching Them unawares, drove Them from the Sky. The <span class=".name">Dæmon</span>, victorious, took the Earth as Its trophy, swallowing the entire world and blackening the Sky with an endless Night.

Generations passed, and Humanity dwindled. The forces of <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> were relentless in their campaign against all that was blessed with <span class=".name">Mysteria</span>'s Light. Cities fell. Libraries burned. Too much, lost to the Fires of <span class=".name">Melimonas</span>.
<!-- page-break -->

# - II -


<span class="drop">B</span>ut all was not lost.

When Humanity was at its lowest, a Hero was chosen by <span class=".name">Mysteria</span> Themself. She was a Knight, and her name was Palomina.

<span class=".name">Mysteria</span> appeared before Palomina, diminished, but still brilliant in Their Glory. And <span class=".name">Mysteria</span> spake unto Palomina,
"Harken well, Child, for Humanity is not yet lost. Thou art their Final Hope."

And <span class=".name">Mysteria</span> bestowed upon Palomina a Gift, a Spear infused with Their own Light.

And <span class=".name">Mysteria</span> instructed, "Thou shalt take My Light to the highest point and loose it upon the darkened Sky.

"Should its aim be true, and its bearer pure of heart, it shall strike down the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span> and drive It from this place."

Thus spake <span class=".name">Mysteria</span>.
<!-- page-break -->

# - III -


<span class="drop">A</span>nd so Palomina set out towards the tallest peak, steadfast in her duty.

But <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> sensed <span class=".name">Mysteria</span>'s Light and set upon Palomina with Its forces. Wave after wave of enemies drained Palomina of her strength. And just when Palomina felt she could continue no longer, she met the Merchant.

But this was no ordinary Merchant. It was none other than the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span> Itself, disguised as a mere traveler.

"Ho, Knight," called <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> to the beleaguered Palomina, "you seem troubled, friend. Perhaps I may provide something to ease your passage?"

And <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> presented her with a second Spear, infused with hidden Dark Intent.

"This shall aid in your quest! Of that, I am certain!"

Thus spake <span class=".name">Melimonas</span>.

Palomina, being none the wiser, accepted the gift, and bade the Merchant farewell. The forces of <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> thinned as Palomina's travel continued, and though she thought it because of her newfound strength, it was simply that <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> knew It had already won.

Upon reaching the highest peak, Palomina called out, "<span class=".name">Melimonas</span>!

"I have beaten back your forces! I have reached this, the highest peak in all the land, and I will do battle unto you!"
<!-- page-break -->

# - IV -


<span class="drop">M</span><span class=".name">elimonas</span> simply laughed.

"Thou seek'st to do battle unto Me? Fine then, Knight. Level thy Spear at Me, and We shall see who is victorious."

Thus spake <span class=".name">Melimonas</span>.

But when Palomina took <span class=".name">Mysteria</span>'s Spear, and heft it above her head to loose it, it shattered as glass in her hands.

For <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> was clever. It knew <span class=".name">Mysteria</span> would not let any of Its dark forces wield the Spear. So little by little, bit by bit, its tainted Spear had corrupted Palomina, making her appear as one of the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span>'s own.

<span class=".name">Melimonas</span> erupted into raucous laughter as the mortified Palomina scrambled to pick up the scattered Shards. "See now how thou hold'st no power over Me! But thou hast another Spear, dost thou not? Perhaps it shall be more effective!"

Thus spake <span class=".name">Melimonas</span>.

Palomina, out of options, and not knowing the Dark Spear's blighted provenance, heft it high above her head. But when she tried to loose the Spear, it held onto her with tendrils of oozing Darkness and pulled her high into the Sky.
<!-- page-break -->

# - V -


<span class="drop">"F</span>oolish little insect!" exclaimed the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span>, "Thou didst think to best Me?

"Now thou art Mine, suspended in My Night! Now thou shalt watch as thy precious Humanity burns, along with the rest of <span class=".name">Mysteria</span>'s wretched Light!"

Thus spake <span class=".name">Melimonas</span>.

And <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> besieged Palomina's mind with horrors. Towns abandoned, children orphaned. Plague and famine rampant. Death ascendant.

The fall of Humanity itself.

Palomina struggled against the tide of omens as they threatened to pull her under, deep into a sea of her own despair.
<!-- page-break -->

# - VI -


<span class="drop">"H</span>arken!" The voice of <span class=".name">Mysteria</span> whispered from the Shards of Light, "Fear not, Child, for Humanity is not yet lost.

"Thou art their final hope, and thou shalt take what remains of My Light, and loose it upon the Sky. It shall strike the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span> as a thousand piercing bolts, and We shall drive It from this place."

Thus spake <span class=".name">Mysteria</span>.

Slowly, Palomina understood. She needed not a Spear, but the Light, and the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span> was much closer than before.
<!-- page-break -->

# - VII -


<span class="drop">"D</span><span class=".name">æmon</span>!" Palomina exclaimed, her resolve returning. "If you seek our destruction, then I must not waver!"

And with a mighty throw, she loosed the thousands of Shards from her grasp.

<span class=".name">Melimonas</span> screamed, as <span class=".name">Mysteria</span>'s searing Light tore through Its Night, dotting its surface with a thousand-thousand bright, shimmering stars. As <span class=".name">Mysteria</span>'s Light poured through, Palomina brandished the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span>'s own Dark Spear, and the Shadow was rent from the Spear by the Glory of the Light.

Palomina took aim at the <span class=".name">Dæmon Lord</span>, and once again loosed herself upon It, tearing a shimmering hole straight through <span class=".name">Melimonas</span> and Its Night. It was large enough to dwarf all others but the Sun itself.

But even cleansed the Dark Spear refused to release Palomina's hand. And thus did it carry Palomina far beyond the Sky, and deep into the Night she pierced.

Palomina would never again return to the Earth, but she left behind the wound rent through the <span class=".name">Dæmon</span>'s Night; a brilliant Moon to mark her sacrifice.
<!-- page-break -->

# - VIII -


<span class="drop">N</span>ever again was <span class=".name">Melimonas</span>' Night so Dark that <span class=".name">Mysteria</span> could not banish it from the Sky.

And as a new dawn rose upon Humanity, they all sang the name of their Hero:

"<span class=".name">Palomina</span>! Wielder of the Spears of Light and Dark! The Goddess of the Moon!

"May she ever return to us in Glory, and in Light!"

Thus spake Humanity, and thus was <span class=".name">Palomina</span> ascendant.
