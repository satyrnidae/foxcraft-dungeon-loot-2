# Foreword

The great land of Mysteria is divided into many holdings and lands of quite diverse natures. In this tome I will detail six of the most renowned and central holdings.
<br>

# Foxtrot

The first of these is the cradle of Mysterian civilization: Foxtrot. This swampy region in the northwest began as a simple underground bunker where the Founders made their home. When these individuals began to disperse throughout Mysteria, the hold was claimed by the First Founder, Mayor Boredfox, who built an elegant house just to the west of the bunker entrance.

In the following months, the Mayor created a melon farm and a guest house while continuing to decorate her own. Eventually, the remaining Founders and others who journeyed to Mysteria asked for and were summarily granted property in the hold. While most of these houses are residential, there is also a minecart station and Shriner's Insurance Company.
<br>

# Thalbodir

Directly down the road leading away from the Foxtrot Mire is Thalbodir Hold. This region is mostly a desert of sand that forms a rise upon which Thalbodir Citadel sits. Beginning as a church that was built over a long period of time by Mayor Boredfox, Shriner of Shriner's Insurance Company, and Chickenny (who was not one of the Founders), Thalbodir Citadel slowly evolved into a thriving, walled town.

Instead of the normal coarse, sandy ground of the region, Thalbodir sits upon soil and grass. Several of the houses are businesses like the wool shop, the bakery, and the Thalbodir Bank while others may be residential. Twin golems roam the area within the massive Thalbodiri walls, which are a wonderous sight to see.
<br>

# Downsville

Northeast of Thalbodir is the Downsville Hold, which seems rather plain on the surface with a small, stone-walled crop and a road shadowed on either side by large trees. There is one rather large, wooden house in the southernmost reaches of the hold belonging to Samn.

The crisscrossing roads of this region lead north to a wide, hollow tree that is planted above the entrance to the Undercity, where Superkiwi resides. The Undercity is a huge cavern in which there is an animal farm, a wheat farm, and passage to Samn's abode, and the passage to Superkiwi's mines. Also located in the Undercity is a tunnel that leads to one of two places.
<br>

# Deinotown

The first of these possible places is Deinotown, in the middle of Desolate Hold, a mountain region that began as a simple subterranean house in a mountainside that quickly grew to hold a crop and mines. Aboveground, directly outside of this residence, there was an animal farm and a road leading to a dock and to where the entrance of Deinotown would one day be located. The decline of this region began when the owner, Shriner of Shriner's Insurance, sold it to Yergaderga and Deino. These two fashioned their houses a little bit down the road from the original house and began the process of building a wall. The town, not so well-lit, was soon under attack by many Mysterian beasts. The dock and the original house, the subterranean section having been long ago blocked off, fell into disrepair. Eventually, the Founders descended on the cobblestone town and eviscerated it, destroying everything the creator held dear. Deino then built a rather sad church in the ruins of his city, and, when said holy place was defiled by creepers and outshone by Thalbodir Church, he left Mysteria, possibly forever.
<br>

# Zephyria

The other hold the tunnel from the Undercity can lead to is Zephyria Hold, where I reside amongst so many constructions. Aboveground in the swampy plain of Zephyria Hold from west to east are the Zephyria Ranch (sadly unpopulated) the twin towers of Zephyria, the smaller to the south and the larger in the north, the melon and chicken huts along with the main body of Zephyria, and the forest and cropland. In the main body of Zephyria, a massive cobblestone, mostly subterranean construction, there is the courtyard, several tunnels shooting off into the Zephyrian Ravine, and a nether portal room. A passage in the Hall leads to the cropland and the four other passages, from east to west, lead to the original Zephyria House Base Camp I, the passage to and from Desolate and Downsville Holds, the passage to the Zephyrian Caves (an almost infinitely expansive cave system), and the main entrance to the Upper Caves. My true house is Base Camp II, located within the main cave system. A catacomb many meters beneath the surface runs its twisting way through Mysteria, eventually reaching the mines of Desolate Hold. Four golems roam the lower and upper reaches and there is a stronghold located in the caves that can be reached across the Zephyrian Bridge.

Zephyria Hold began as a mere cave system with a large bowl-shaped depression easily seen from aboveground. The area slowly evolved from this to what it is today, a sectioned, subterranean mansion. The ravine was the first part of the system to be lit; this job was done by Mayor Boredfox and me before the rest of the caves were lit. There is another depression to the east of the main house connected through the caves that has been claimed for Zephyria.
<br>

# The Valley of Peace

The final hold to be detailed in this book is the Valley of Peace, a tundra and mountainous region to the east of Desolate Hold. This valley was originally claimed by Flyfroger, who was soon after evicted by Macedon Zero in a semi-hostile takeover. Macedon Zero allowed Shriner to build his second house within it. This house was later abandoned, and Macedon Zero's territory grew to encompass this home within the mountain and its counterpart across the Frozen Lake, connected via a massive bridge.

In the northernmost part of this region, there is a subholding of mountains whereupon lies the home of Yergaderga, a large mansion of stone. The Valley of Peace is connected to the Desolate Hold by the Guarded Road, a path with guard towers on either side. This road constitutes its own subholding, The Plains, where are located the courthouse, the second home of Deino, and the home of Gunnystryker. The region between Desolate Hold and Zephyrian Hold is the Broken Meadow subholding, where wars have been fought. And thus are the positions and histories of the Mysterian Holds.
