# The Peaceful

Throughout the many holdings of Mysteria, there is a wide variety of creatures that wander the world peacefully, grazing on the plains and refraining from attacking individuals.

## Sheep

One of the greatest resources of Mysteria is the sheep. Sheep range in colors, from white to black, and can also be dyed with the juices of certain plants and objects. One can use sheers to remove the wool from a sheep, and this material can also be dyed any color in the spectrum.

## Pigs

Another wild beast of Mysteria is the common pig, a tough-skinned creature that can be killed for its meat; up to three servings of pork can be collected in this manner.

## Cows

The cow is another large, common beast. Cows are hunted for two reasons - their skin can be used as leather for armor and book covers and their meat can also be taken as food.

## Chickens

Chickens wander the Mysterian wilds, laying eggs from which the young of their kind hatches. These birds are killed for their feathers, which are useful in arrows, and for their meat.

## Squid

Squid can be found in bodies of water all across Mysteria, and can be found in abundance in the ocean to the north and swamp to the south. All these creatures, with the notable exception of the squid, can be bred through the use of wheat, of all things.

Also, all of the various meats obtained from these creatures can be cooked in a furnace.
<br>

# The Hostile

Hostile creatures make their homes beneath the surface of Mysteria and come out in great numbers at nighttime.

## Creepers

The first of these beings is the greatest threat, the creeper. Known widely as a destructive, malignant force, the creeper will explode on contact with anything they believe threatens them. If they are killed before this act, gunpowder can be salvaged from their remains. This volatile material can be used in tnt, which in turn can be used in defense of one's home or in order to begin a quarry. The best method in which to attack a creeper is to charge it, strike, and then retreat out of explosion range. Repeat this until it falls.

## Spiders

Another hostile creature of Mysteria is the deadly giant spider. This arachnid can be slaughtered for its string which can be used in tripwire, for decoration, or for a multitude of other tools. Spiders are some of the hardest creatures of Mysteria to destroy. Though they are easy if you can sustain a couple of wounds. Simply strike than leap back out of range, though the reach of jumping spiders is long, or use a weapon to block its attacks and strike between pounces.

## Zombies

Another hostile creature of Mysteria is the zombie. These undead creatures are relentless in their attacks and often come forth in hordes rather than attack as individuals like many of the other beasts. They can take several hits, but should fall relatively easily to the seasoned fighter. Zombies corpses can be looted for rotten flesh, which, though it causes intense hunger pains, can be consumed by the desperate.

## Skeletons

Skeletons are the bony remnants of people who died in Mysteria. They use bows to initiate long-ranged combat, and this can make them a danger to even veteran adventurers. They can be taken out from a range with bows, a technique that destroys their considerable advantage, or they can be rushed and destroyed with a few quick blows (though the latter method will likely get someone shot with an arrow at least once). Destroyed skeletons obviously leave behind bones, which are useful in bone meal, a substance that can be used to quickly grow crops and trees.

## Slimes

Deep in the bowels of the world live the slime. These slimy, green creatures hop around in caves and low places, killing adventurers brave or foolish enough to venture that far into the ground. The unarmed warrior should simply keep away from a slime, since the creatures are extremely resilient to attack. If a warrior is armed, however, he can strike the slime between its deadly jumps. When the slime seems to have been killed, a wise adventurer should not sheath his weapon, for the creature will then burst into smaller versions of itself. This process can occur twice before the creatures are destroyed forever. Slime balls, useful ingredients in potion-making, can be harvested from a slime's remains.

## Silverfish

Hiding in the walls of ancient underground strongholds are the silverfish. These creatures worm their way out of the walls and attack in large swarms whenever potential prey comes by. Silk can be harvested from a dead silverfish's body.

Some of the aforementioned creatures drop iron ingots and tools or bows. Occasionally, a record can be found on the dead body of a creeper.

# The Otherworldly: The Nether

## Pigmen

On the thick shelves of the nether, the most common creature is the zombie pigman. Formerly pigmen of Mysteria, these beasts were long ago enslaved as undead and forced to forever wander the nether. Pigmen are fiercely protective of one another, so if one is attacked, every zombie pigman in sight will converge upon the attacker, slashing wildly with gold swords. After the zombie is felled, its rotten flesh can be harvested as food for the desperate. Since zombie pigmen love gold, small amounts of this precious metal can likely be found in their possession.

## Ghasts

Though pigmen are the most common creatures of the nether, the most dangerous are the ghasts. Massive floating jellyfish-like beasts that spit exploding balls of fire at anything they don't recognize, ghasts can blast someone straight of the shelves and into the scalding lava far below. They are easy to kill with a bow (two well-placed shots should do the creature in) or by deflecting their fireballs back toward them. Just one of these will down the ghast. Upon its death, a ghast disappears in a cloud of smoke, leaving gunpowder (useful in tnt) and sometimes a ghast's tear (useful in potionmaking) behind.

## Magma Cubes

Another dangerous creature of the nether is the molten slime, the counterpart of Mysteria's slimes, which can burn anyone they come into contact with. Magma cream, an ingredient for alchemy, can be scooped from their dead flesh.

## Blazes

The final creature of the nether is the blaze. These floating beasts of flame can blast three bolts of fire between each recharging period at foes. The creatures rarely appear individually, so rushing and demolishing them with melee weapons is difficult. The best way to kill them is with a bow, but in their weak state between volleys, any weapon can fairly easily down them. Blazes leave behind blaze rods, small cylinders that rotate around them while in combat. These rods can be used to create eyes of ender and potion stands.
<br>

# The Otherworldly: The End

## The Ender Dragon

The End is another dimension filled to the brim with hostile creatures. Most of these are endermen, which swarm across the floating islands of the End, zeroing in on any who glance into their eyes. Once again, however, the most common creatures and the most dangerous creatures are two different things entirely. The mighty enderdragon swoops through the void skies of the End, crashing down with its considerable bulk atop any who deign to stand against it. It is said that this beast, with its powerful regeneration and evasive flying, can only be killed by strange explosive devices set upon the obsidian pillars of the End.

## Endermen

The endermen below grovel at the feet of the enderdragons. With their tall forms and purple, glowing eyes, these creatures are quite imposing. They teleport around their foes, any who look them in the eye, in a confusing manner, forcing the victim to whirl around quickly and repeatedly to avoid being taken down from behind. The easiest way to kill an enderman is to climb up a four meter pillar on high ground and draw the enderman closer. Then, one needs only to stab at the being below until it falls. When it does so, its enderpearl can be taken from its corpse. The enderpearl is a strange gem that can be thrown to teleport the user to wherever it shattered. Also, a pearl can be used in an eye of ender. Endermen in the end are the hardest to slay, because there are thousands of them scattered across each island. In the End, it is better to simply avoid them. However, endermen also sometimes warp to Mysteria, where they can be downed much quicker.
<br>

# The Friendly

## Wolves

Wolves can be found all across the continent of Mysteria. These creatures are usually docile, roaming through forested regions, occasionally taking down a sheep. When one attempts to kill a wolf, every wolf in the pack will converge upon him and maul him. When a docile wolf is fed a certain amount of bones, it will become friendly and follow its new master around, attacking his foes, as long as said foe is not a creeper.

## Ocelots

The ocelots of Foxtrot are also docile. They are easily scared and will run away as fast as they can if approached. However, should an adventurer stand perfectly still, an ocelot may approach him. At this point, if the cat is fed a fish, it will follow the adventurer in much the same way as a wolf.

## Villagers and Golems

The human villagers of Thalbodir and Downsville are also quite friendly, and will trade with an adventurer. Unfortunately, these people are the prime victims of zombie onslaughts. Iron and snow golems are constructs created by adventurers and villagers. Iron golem specimens will occasionally bestow upon a villager child a rose flower. Both types of golems will defend their creator against foes.
<br>

# The Conclusion

Many creatures of varying types live in Mysteria. While some are quite hostile, several species simply graze in the fields and ignore adventurers, simply running away should someone strike them. Other beasts are peaceful usually, but will attack when struck. Yet more live outside the walls of dimension in the Nether and the End. Almost all of these creatures leave behind useful objects and materials and some can be bred to begin livestock farms. Thus, it can be concluded that Mysteria is a land full of resources and beneficial creatures, and beasts dark and deadly.
