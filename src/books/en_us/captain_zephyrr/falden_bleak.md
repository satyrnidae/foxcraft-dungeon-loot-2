<style class=".title">A Story by Captain Zephyrr</style>
<br>
Falden sat his companion down before the fire, attempting to ward away the frost that still remained on the traveler's cloak.

"May I ask why you were journeying through the Valley of Peace in such wintry weather?" Falden inquired, passing the time while the other man defrosted. When the traveler didn't answer, he shuffled away to get his guest something warm to eat and drink.

Finally, in a hoarse voice, almost a whisper, the traveler replied, "I had not accounted for the blizzard. I thank you much for dragging me out of it."

"Ah, the blizzard. You aren't the only man who has been caught unawares by such tumultuous weather. Many men I know have ceased to be on the coldest of nights, and not many of them were fools." Falden stated grimly, returning with a tray of steaming hot chicken breasts. "So, where you headed off to, anyway, friend?"

The traveler grimaced slightly at the title Falden had applied to him, but he answered anyway, responding, "I was traveling to Thalbodir, for I heard that it was a wondrous sight indeed to see."

"Thalbodir? That's to the west - and the far west, too. Maybe a day's journey from where you started out from, but at least a week from here. I am afraid to tell you that you have been moving steadily north."

"Northward? No, no, I am sure that I was going east! My compass cannot lie to me - that would be absurd."

Falden took a look at the compass in the man's outstretched arm. Sure enough, the direction he pointed it toward, and the direction he had been traveling in, was displayed as east. However, Falden had learned at a tender age to read the stars and knew for certain that east on the compass was definitely north. The man was intrigued, assuredly, but he shook the thoughts of adventure and the open road from his head, remembering his vow. Therefore, he turned the conversation back to Thalbodir.

"You have heard right, traveler, about Thalbodir. It is indeed a remarkable sight to behold. A massive, beautiful wall and an oasis the size of none I have ever seen within a desert."

"Just hearing you tell of it makes it sound amazing - I cannot wait to glimpse it with my own eyes!" the traveler said, a sparkle returned to his eye.

"You shall if I have any say in it!" Falden grinned, shrugging aside any reservations he held for breaking his vow never to journey again.

"I could not -" the traveler began, but he was cut off by Falden's cry of, "But I will go nonetheless!" The former adventurer smiled widely, seeing the acceptance in the traveller's eyes.

"Make yourself at home, my friend, my Ltwe! I will be your guide on this sojourn, and, if you wish, we shall leave on the morn! Thalbodir awaits!"
