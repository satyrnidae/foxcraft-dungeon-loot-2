1. Close any exits such as the mine shaft in your house so that the villager can't wander through and get lost or die.
2. DOORS ARE YOUR ENEMY! The villager will almost certainly go outside in the daytime, and may not be able to reach a safe haven by nightfall. Zombies will slaughter him. Doors should probably be replaced with iron doors opened using levers or buttons, NOT pressure plates.
3. If the area is small or narrow, don't spawn the villager there, because he may suffocate.
4. COMMON SENSE - Be careful with weapons around the villager. Don't accidentally murder him.
