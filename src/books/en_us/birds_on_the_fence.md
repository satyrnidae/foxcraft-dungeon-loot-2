# Birds on the Fence

_One little bird sitting on the fence_
_A second bird came up to dance_
_He danced his dance, he tried his best_
_But the first little bird was not impressed_
_She told him that his dance was swell_
_And bid him farewell._
---
_Two little birds sitting on the fence_
_A third bird came right up to dance_
_He danced a little bit better than the last_
_But the first bird thought that it lacked class_
_She lied and said his dance was swell_
_And bid him farewell._
---
_Three little birds sitting on the fence_
_A fourth bird came right up to dance_
_He danced a little bit better than the rest_
_But the first bird was still not impressed_
_She told him that his dance was swell_
_And bid him farewell._
---
_Four little birds sitting on the fence_
_A fourth bird came right up to dance_
_He danced a dance much better than the rest_
_And the first little bird was quite impressed_
_She told him that his dance was swell_
_And both of them were well._
