# - Foreword -

The purpose of these manuals is to educate my fellow adventurers on the aspects of fiends and aberrations, so that they may better eradicate them. This manual details the animated remains of our bones, the Skeleton.
<br>
# - Appearances -

   Skeletons share many of their behaviors of materialization with their brethren, the zombies. Appearing to assemble from the darkness, skeletons will not propagate their curse to Testificate villagers.

   They are easily located by sound, as their rattling bones betray them. It seems that necromantic residue allows the skeleton its mobility, since it lacks muscle or tendons to move itself. Like their still-fleshed counterparts, skeletons are able to utilize weaponry and armor from their victims. Unlike the zombie, however, the skeleton prefers to attack at range, and I have never seen one without a bow in its clutches. Furthermore, sword on bone favors the sword, as it interrupts their pull as well as causing heavy damage.

   I have been told of a darker, more deadly variant of the skeleton from the Nether, but that hellish realm is not my area of expertise. My colleague Arturia says that the skeletons there roam the Soul Sand wastes and the Nether Fortresses, and that the Nether Fortress variant carries a stone sword rather than a bow. I will expound upon this if I find the time to visit that gloomy place.
<br>
# - Behavior -

   Skeletons are quite alert to their surroundings, but still require a line of sight to pursue a foe. They will sense a human within sixteen paces, and openly engage at that distance. They only fire on someone within twelve or so paces, likely to conserve their ammunition. If their quarry attempts a retreat, a skeleton will pursue them until such time as they escape a range of twenty paces.

   Skeletons are not very bright, and may shoot one of their brethren mid-pursuit. This has resulted in many an entertaining spectacle, as one can watch the two duel to the death. There is also the rare phenomenon in which a creeper is slain by a skeleton's bow, causing it to expel a record! I have found no satisfactory explanation as to why this happens, so feel free to guess yourself.

   Sometimes, skeletons may be seen riding spiders about the open fields, and on floors and walls of caves. The skeletal jockey is more powerful than either the spider or the skeleton alone, combining the ranged attack of the skeleton with the spider's wall-climbing skills. It is of some comfort to me that oftentimes a mounted spider can accidentally crush its rider to death against the ceiling as it climbs, which is the preferable outcome to fighting one on its own.

   Occasionally one may see a lone skeletal horse on a field during a thunderstorm. I stress that you should never approach one of these unawares. It may be waiting to summon the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse: a company of mounted skeletal riders, fast, armored, and deadly accurate. These are best avoided unless you too are similarly outfitted.
<br>
# - Mechanics -

   A normal skeleton's bow can kill an unarmored person in four to six hits. The variable strengths of their pulls cause this inexact measurement. They are undead, meaning that a sword of smiting is best used in dealing with them, as well as the reversal of harming and healing potions' effects on them. As the sun rises, they will seek to find water, shade, or a helmet, as sunlight dissolves the necromancy keeping them mobile.

   Swords of gold and wood will kill one in five to six strikes; stone and iron require four to five, and diamond need only three hits to kill a skeleton. I put variables in this book due to arrows' knock-back, which may allow a skeleton time for its wounds to regenerate.
<br>
# - Spoils -

   Skeletons most often drop some of their ammunition, up to two usable arrows. The rest are often damaged in any struggle, as they are aged and brittle.

   Skeletons also relinquish as many as two of their intact bones, which may be used to fertilize one's crops, lighten a wool's dye, or tame wild hounds.

   Approximately one in forty will relinquish their bow, and one in five of these may have been bestowed to deal a small amount of extra damage. If a skeleton is seen wearing armor, it may drop this as well.
<br>
# - Afterword -

   One day I hope to find whoever is responsible for creating these abhorrent creatures. But until then, I must be content with slaying any and all of them that I see… and now that you are properly forewarned, so too may you.
