# - Foreword -

   The purpose of these manuals is to educate my fellow adventurers on the aspects of fiends and aberrations, so that they may better eradicate them. This manual details the oversized arachnids of the Overworld, the spider.
<br>
# - Appearances -

   Spiders skulk in dark places, emerging from the blackness as though born from it. Like the other fiends of this world, they will not appear within fifteen paces of a placed torch, although they may wander freely into the light once they have spawned.

   Spiders are easily identified even in the dark by two traits: the first is their hissing while they exhale from their book lungs, and the second is their glowing red eyes, which pierce through the dark in even the deepest caves.

   Some spiders spawn under the influence of potion-like effects, seemingly an adaptation to harsher regions, especially those where humans and spiders have long been at odds. These spiders may be faster, hit harder, or even be practically invisible, their eyes appearing to float disembodied through the air.

   Spiders vary slightly in size, but the variation is most noticeable in cave spiders. Found infesting mineshafts and the deepest caves, they have bodies about half as large as the average spider and a bite many times as poisonous. The lingering poison can subdue even the most heavily armored adventurer. I strongly recommend stocking up on palliatives like buckets of milk before braving the areas these spiders frequent.
<br>
# - Behavior -

   Unlike the other aberrations, spiders have a habit of appearing uninterested in fighting during the daytime. Some of my colleagues in the field assume this is an intentional behavior, but I believe it is more of an accidental one. You see, the multiple eyes of a spider are well adapted to dark caves and moonlight, but ill-suited to the bright rays of the sun. Thus, I hypothesize that the spider might simply not be able to see nearby humans at all in the bright light of the sun! When the spider enters a sufficiently shaded area to regain its senses, or when it is attacked directly, it will not hesitate to fight.

   Spiders have the unique ability to scale vertical surfaces as though they were flat ground. They can often be seen in caves clinging close to the ceiling. They can also pass through cobwebs easily, allowing them quick access to any prey they may have ensnared.

   Both spiders and cave spiders are immune to poisoning.

   As I mentioned in my book on the skeleton, spiders are occasionally seen sporting a skeletal rider. These teams should generally be avoided, but it is a small comfort that oftentimes the spider will crush its rider to death against the ceiling as it climbs, which leads to a fast end to the partnership.
<br>
# - Mechanics -

   The danger in spiders does not lie so much in their attacks; usually one can survive up to nine direct hits from a spider if they are unarmored. The issue lies in their attack pattern: they lunge at their prey as a fox would pounce on a rabbit. One spider alone is not too much of a problem, but a group can quickly overwhelm their quarry.

   This is especially true of cave spiders, which almost always congregate in large groups and can envenom a person with a short-lived but potent poison that can strip someone down within an inch of their life.

   Swords of wood, gold, stone, and copper can kill a spider in four strikes, and a cave spider in three. Iron and diamond both require three strikes, though diamond and iron can consistently kill a cave spider in two. Netherite-infused swords can kill either in two strikes.
<br>
# - Spoils -

   Spiders often drop one or two pieces of silken string about two thirds of the time. This silk is essential in the crafting of bows, fishing rods, and tripwire traps. It can also be woven into a good substitute for wool, though you may find it easier and more efficient to domesticate some sheep!

   Spiders can also drop spider eyes about one third of the time, which can be used as an emergency food source, though they will poison you. They may alternatively be fermented for use in various poultices and potions.
<br>
# - Afterword -

   One day I hope to find whoever is responsible for creating these abhorrent creatures. But until then, I must be content with slaying any and all of them that I see… and now that you are properly forewarned, so too may you.
