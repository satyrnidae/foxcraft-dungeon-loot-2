# - Foreword -

   The purpose of these manuals is to educate my fellow adventurers on the aspects of fiends and aberrations, so that they may better eradicate them. This manual is on the undead remains of our kind.
<br>
# - Appearances -

   Zombies seem to materialize out of thin air, but only while the surrounding area is pitch black. Personal testing has confirmed that the beasts will not appear within fifteen paces of a placed torch.

   They all share similar physiology with us, as they are mostly reanimated corpses of our kind, *Homo sapiens playiensus*. Some are also of our cousins *H. sapiens testificatus*; these varieties are more likely to appear in infected villages. Another similar variety appears in the Nether, infecting the local populations of *Sus sapiens* and occasionally members of *S. infernus*; the infectious curse responsible for zombification can seemingly transfer between mammalian lines, suggesting a common, ancestral root.

   Some zombies wear remnants of armor or carry weaponry. Most still retain enough motor function to pick up the equipment of their past victims and utilize it. Woe to those slain with their diamond swords!
<br>
# - Behavior -

   Zombies, once observed from a safe distance, appear to be quite listless. They shuffle and groan with the weight of their decomposing tissue, and wander blindly. Upon seeing a suitable victim, they begin growling aggressively and attempt to bludgeon anyone they see. This is a minor nuisance at best for the seasoned swordsman, as armor greatly reduces their combat strength.

   Doors are also unsafe; the zombie seems to know there is game afoot inside doors, and will rip them away to get at anyone behind them. A sturdy door of iron will protect against any and all attacks.
<br>
# - Mechanics -

   Zombies are capable of strikes on an unarmored person that prove fatal after four hits.

   Their skin appears to have toughened with age, and they are slightly shielded from slashing and piercing damage as a result. Fire, the sun, and healing potions can bypass this armor entirely. Being undead, they are dealt best with a sword that is blessed by Mysteria's grace, or a bow that bestows the sun's might upon its arrows.

   As the sun rises, the zombie must either wear a helm or seek shade to avoid being cleansed by its might. Swords of gold and wood will kill a zombie in six strikes, those of stone require five, iron will kill in four, and diamond requires three strikes to kill.
<br>
# - Spoils -

   Zombies commonly drop their rotted flesh, up to two edible shavings. Eating it is not advised, as it is vile and weakens one's constitution.

   The rarer drops tend to be whatever armor they may be seen wearing, carrots, potatoes, ingots of iron, and swords or shovels of iron. Where the zombie acquired these items is still a mystery, as I have seen one such zombie drop a carrot without a farm in sight!
