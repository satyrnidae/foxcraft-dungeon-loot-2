It's been one month since the incident
When a shadow, tall and lean
Entered my house in a rainstorm
Teleporting, thus unseen.

It was content to stay awhile
Rummaging through the room
Touching all of its contents
Slowly spelling out our doom.

'Twas not by mere coincidence
That later in the night
A burning log pulled from the hearth
Cast my abode alight.

With my house the inferno took
My dear and loving wife;
Only I, burnéd and bruised
Could escape with my life.

And here I still stay, my world destroyed,
Cast in internal strife,
Waiting, watching for that shadow
To spare me from <font color="dark_red" shadow="#EE993300">||this life.||</font>
