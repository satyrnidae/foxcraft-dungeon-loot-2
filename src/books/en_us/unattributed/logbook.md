# Day 25, Year 2

Let it be known that on day 25 of the second year (day 305 overall) this journal was opened to commemorate the comings and goings of this second year of survival in this place.
For clarity, a week is defined as seven days; a month is four weeks, or 28 days, and a year is 10 months, or 280 days.

<center><-></center>

Shortly before the year began, a traveler of ill intent descended upon the village.  As the survivors slept, this dastardly individual detonated the stores of TNT in the chest room, and flooded the farms and yard quite thoroughly before making a dash to escape.  Such a possibility was deemed so unlikely that no preparations had been made beforehand for dealing with this, and when Marianna awoke to find the destruction she was left very distraught.

An effort was started immediately to repair the damage this individual inflicted, and into the next year and through the first month, the water was cleared, the farms restored, and the animals returned to their pens.  Some structures, such as the original fencing and the stilt house, were so badly destroyed by fire that they were removed for future rebuilding.

The perpetrator could not be found, was presumed dead, and a burial area was made far apart from the cemetery.  None mourned their passing.

On day 305, the cleanup effort was completed on the internal structure of the base, and farm production was able to return to normal.  The chest room was even expanded to allow for more storage space in the future.  Unfortunately, the contents of the chest room, including many diamonds, and several books including the OSHA standards handbook, a short story, and the account of the first year were lost in the explosion, and could not be recovered.

# Day 95, Year 2

On day 375, the keeper of records, Tai Kitsune, died in a scuffle at the cemetery while distracted. No remains were found.
<br>

# Day 242, Year 2

Day 522, new Keeper Kenny Chick reporting. The supplies chests have been nearly completely filled, and food is in no way a concern once more. Some structures, such as the fences, have been repaired. Others, such as the stilt house, have been left as they were, with no repair efforts intended. OSHA disbanded shortly after the destruction, and no organization has yet come to take its place. No raids into the nether have occurred since the incursion, and the funeral for Tai Kitsune was attended by few; most are still too shaken by the events prior to attend. The graveyard also still is in need of repairs to the fencing.
