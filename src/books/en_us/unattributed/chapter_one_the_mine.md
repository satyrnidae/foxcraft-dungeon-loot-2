For years all I had ever known was the constant hum-drum of the suburban society. Pre-planned communities and the iron standard were plastered upon my eyes the minute I opened them. It was almost as if big brother wanted me to believe the entire world could fit within the confinds of the cobblestone walls I called home. No one ever spoke about the outside world, and looking back, I bet half the people I knew were as clueless as I was. At that point in time, what was ahead of me was so monstrously inconceivable that not even the greatest of scholars could not begin to explain what would ly ahead for me. But I, being the foolish child I was, jumped without looking. Only the Lord Notch knew exactly what kind of a mistake that was...

Chapter One:
The Mine

---
As the days grew longer and summer approached, I eagarly awaited my 13th birthday. The fine print of Thalbodir legislature states that when a boy turns the age of 13, He is permitted to enter the mines in the center of the city. For me this was beyond important. My father used to come home everyday from working diligantly within the mines. He would sit down at the dinner table and tell wonderful stories from his long day at work. He loved his job with a passion. He made the mines sound like the most interesting place in the entire world. As imaginable, the joy I felt on my 13th birthday was out of this world. I remember waking up that day and racing down the stairs to meet my father at the door. I couldn't wait to go with him to the mines. For him, it was just another day at work. But for me, it was the day that my whole childhood had culminated to. So we departed from the house and headed right to the mine. Every step I took seemed to be a thousand times slower than the last.
