I rushed through the cave, looking for my torches. They had all disappeared, or maybe I was in a new part of the the long, intertwining system I was going through. I sprinted through, becoming hungry but not stopping. Before long, I came across an abandoned shaft. Such places never seemed inviting for me, so mysteriously empty and hauntingly quiet.

Maybe...

I pulled out my flint and steel, rusty and decrepit. I tried to ignite a fire on the supporting beams of the shaft. It was a dangerous idea and unlikely to be fruitful, but it was my only hope. I needed light to make any progress.

I crashed my kindler against the wood. It was cold, slimy even. I continued to try to light the planks, but to no avail. I let out a strong, whooping cough. I had been coughing since I had begun mining coal in these catacombs. My cough persisted but I thought nothing of it.

Then, footsteps.

I looked behind me for reasons I to this day don't understand. I couldn't see anything, so why did I look? The air behind me was foggy and dark. But, I swear I saw these, I would call them purple, lights in the background. Some kind of... eyes.

I picked up my bag to run, but my diary fell out. I reached down to grab it and the pages fumbled in my sooty, trembling hands. I threw it in my bag and bolted. The noises followed me. I must have been tired and delusional, because I thought I even heard a note block serenade playing in a disturbingly ironic tone. I ran blindly until I saw the faint lights of the stars-an escape from what had become some hellish dream, all so quickly and suddenly. I climbed up the path of grass leading from the opening, a horridly scream-ish noise following me.

I looked behind as I ran, the deep purple lights darting behind me. For the split second I looked, said glowing lights shot across the landscape, as if they were teleporting. Then, I hit a hard surface and fell down. I looked up to see these purple lights right in my face, larger and deeper than ever. I tried to scream for help in that moment of terror.
