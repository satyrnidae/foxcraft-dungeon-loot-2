  In our Minecraftian Government, there are a few things you should know about if you ever want to become the king or the boss of every pixel.

  First, you should know the politics. The pastey, succesful men you see... could be your bright future! The People and the President have the relationship that allows the leader to do what the people need!

  Lesson two is something that every future king or queen will need to know! As a leader of our great blocky world, your work station must be upon the large cloud of blocks. Leaders lay upon these clouds and embrace them as if they were his/her big happy family.

  In conclusion, you should act in an appropriate manner. Do not destroy those who even deserve to be destroyed. Do not interfere with a nice life of cubes. Do not disappoint those who live in your land. If you've got no life because of that, so be it! You will deserve to live your pastey, miserable life for no reason.

  Leadership is vital...
