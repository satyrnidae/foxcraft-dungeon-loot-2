  There was once an ugly little pumpkin. He was really ugly— no, hideous. Your eyes would probably puke and fly away from your non-ugly face! Although, one day a little girl pumpkin named Agetha was strolling along when she saw the hideous pumpkin (who, from now on, is named Uglitha).

  From the moment she laid eyes on Uglitha, she wanted to say only one thing. "My eyes! My beautiful yet unfortunate eyes!" she screamed as she ran away, bawling.

  As Uglitha walked on the street, he found a store that was named "Business that makes terribly hideous pumpkins named Uglitha pretty". Uglitha then went into the store. He was terribly hideous! He was a pumpkin! His name was Uglitha! He was just the guy to do it.

  "I'd like to be beautiful, please," he said with a paper bag on his head.

  "Certainly... that is, if you have the money!" said the unnamed organism.

  Uglitha forgot that he'd been so ugly that he was kicked out of every job he had! He observed the setting around him... There was bodywash, shampoo, a shower, some makeup, a model of the Empire State Building, and a free 'Beauty in a Bottle'! He knew just what he had to do. He quickly went home (with a bag on his head) to his house which he suddenly had, got on his computer which he suddenly had, and got a job at a bubblegum factory which was suddenly there. He got a dollar, and went back to the store.

  "I would like a model of the Empire State Building, please!" he said.

  He threw the model into the sunset and baked a pie. He then shoved it into the organism's face, and became a beautiful creature.
