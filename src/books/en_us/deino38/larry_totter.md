  Larry Totter was a strange senior citizen who lived with his aunt, uncle, and cousin. Larry was turning 171 soon. His cousin was a disgusting, wrinkly, fat potatoe-like creature. His aunt and uncle favored him more than Larry!

  He was sitting in his room when a large hairy man saw Larry's chocolate covered door. He suddenly started licking the door! Finally, after two hours, he licked his fingers and ate the whole door. His 171 year old cousin, 203 year old aunt, and his 43 year old uncle, stared at him looking horrified. It took it's beard off, turning out to be a shiny, bald elf named Bobby!

  "Please save me!" it said. "There's a 65 faced man, a giant worm, and a really skinny, hooded man that floats around sucking out peoples colons that want to harm me!"

  Larry didn't know what do do, so they ate Bobby. The skinny guy and the snake left, but the 65 faced man said something to Larry before he left.

  "I was the one who sent your parents on the 170 year long shopping trip, gave you the mustache shaped scar under your nose, and gave you that invitation to pigwarts 3 years ago," he yelled. Larry forgot about Pigwarts-school where a bearded guy teaches you magic tricks. He thanked the 65 faced man, but he just turned into bisquick. A plume of bisquick arose from the pile.

  "I'm Lord Moldy-s'more," it yelled. Larry was then suddenly at pigwarts.

  Then, Moldy-s'more was a baby. Then, he was big again. Then, he got an army. Then, he killed the head bearded man. Then, 6 parts of his soul were destroyed. Then, he was in a dual with Larry! As they fought with electric sticks, Larry won. Then everything was happy, and Larry got a life.
