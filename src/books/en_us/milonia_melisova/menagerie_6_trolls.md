<style class=".title">Chapter One</style>
<style class=".title"><style class=".symbologie">>-<</style></style>
<style class=".title">Troll Physiology</style>
<br>
   Trolls are squat and scrunchy creatures.  They are wide as a man but only half as tall, give or take a few centimeters.  But do not let their stature lull you into a false sense of security.  At half the size of a human being, they weigh up to five times as much, depending on which mineral they embody.

   For you see, Trolls do not reproduce as the birds and bees do.  Trolls are not born; they arise, clawing out from the very crust of the Earth itself.  Rock, driven to locomotion by some unseen, unknowable force.

   As a result, they do bear a passing resemblance to rocks.  However, they most resemble amphibians; frogs, toads, even fish to some extent.  Geologists believe that perhaps when Trolls first clawed their way from the dark, shadowed depths, they modeled their form after the life they observed, which at the time would have been fish, and maybe a couple part-time land animals.
   
   For you see, Trolls are rocks.  And rocks have been around forever.

<style class=".title">Chapter Two</style>
<style class=".title"><style class=".symbologie">>-<</style></style>
<style class=".title">Troll Behavior</style>
<br>
   Trolls are active by night, excepting those trolls found wandering below the ground, for reasons I shall expand upon in a later section.  Their gangly forelimbs can pick up and throw a variety of objects, and some have been known to equip bows and crossbows.  Trolls are not evil, but antagonistic by nature.  They will pelt you with snowballs.  They will steal your items.  They will laugh at you, yes, AT you, in their grunting little voices.  To a Troll, you are little more than a plaything; less a nuisance, more a nuisee.

   At least until threatened, Trolls will dance and galavant about you, tossing the random egg at your head and stealing your hat to wear.

   Trolls cannot be damaged by swords or axes, so even brandishing your weapon of choice will elicit little more than a delighted chuckle.  The real danger comes when you try to mine near one.

   For remember, Trolls and rocks are one and the same, and rocks don't take too kindly to being smashed up and used as the foundation for somebody's house.  The pickaxe is the only tool which can truly damage a Troll, and upon noticing it, they will enter a bloodied rage, yelling and attacking with the full force of a mobile boulder.  Your only hope is to defeat the angered troll with your pickaxe, as merely having the blasted thing is enough to set a Troll off.

   Trolls have been known to craft armor for themselves, often of wooden plate from trees, or metal secreted from within their own bodies, but they are not beyond taking back what they can salvage from an aggressor.  They will equip every dropped item so long as there is an item to equip.  Not only does this greatly improve the Troll's already excellent armor plating, but it allows the Troll to continue moving about aboveground while the sun is in the sky.

   For unlike skeletons and zombies (though not entirely unlike them, I admit) Trolls do not burn in the sunlight, but are reduced to their non-motive forms once more.  Trolls frozen in stony prisons can be seen dotting the landscape after a thunderstorm, or near collapsed bridges and structures too decayed to truly block the sun's rays.  This is the time at which it is easiest to dispatch a troll, and the reason why they wear armor.  Even armor can only protect them for so long; soon, the stone cask breaks through the casing, rendering the armor ineffective.
