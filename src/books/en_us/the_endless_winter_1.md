# Chapter I

  Liara Thimbleweed was always obsessed with her dreams. She catalogued each one upon waking, and studied her notes and ramblings for common threads and misbegotten omens, hoping that one day she would find something useful, or insightful, or prophetic. She'd actually managed to save a village from a flood once that way, though not even she was sure whether that was fate or simply coincidence. Lately though...

  "...I open my eyes, and it's like I haven't. I can't see my hand in front of my face. The air is damp, and cold as ice. It freezes in my chest, burns on every breath. The ground is... wet, or icy. I don't dare move lest I fall.

  "There's a dreadful silence. It's louder than any silence ought to be. I call out, but my voice feels muffled, as though the air itself abhors the sound. For a time, all I hear is the quickening pace of my own heart.

  "But it's interrupted by a new sound: like a hammer striking an anvil. It's far away, but I creep towards it, each footfall threatening to dash me against the ice. Soon, I hear voices. They're guttural, and aren't speaking any language I can understand. The din begins to lift as I get closer, and soon, I see torchlight shining through a crack, the small beam reflecting off the frozen cave walls in a sparkling glory.

  "You'd think I would think better of it, but something almost compels me to peer through the crack. I hunch down and stare through the small portal. My eyes scream from the sudden brightness, but I don't blink... can't blink. Because looking through the hole, I see Drauven. Dozens of them. Wrapped in thick furs, huddling around makeshift braziers, striking the rock with stolen pickaxes, almost feverish in their search for... something. Me?

  "I scramble to get up, but slip on the frozen floor. I scream as skin tears against the sharp rocks and blood splashes against the ice. The pickaxes stop, and the light from the crack suddenly dims...

  "They've found me.

  "My arm is broken from the fall. Cold and adrenaline dim the pain as I scrabble to right myself. A loud roar is followed by the din of pickaxes against the thin wall separating me from them, but I am upright, and as I start to run down the slippery path I can hear the cave wall crumble behind me. My ears are full of frantic Drauven speech as they pour into my thin tunnel. In my terror, I look back, but I lose my footing. The floor rushes to meet me as I let out a scream..."

  "...And that's when you wake up, isn't it?" Timur interjected.

  Liara stared into the depths of the fire. The rolling coals glowed brightly as the flames licked happily at fresh logs, but Liara pulled her cloak tighter, as though staving off the memory of the cold. "Yeah," she finally replied. "That's when I wake up."

  Timur poked at the fire with his pike, sending fluttering sparks skyward. He remembered the look in her eyes when he rushed to her side as she screamed herself awake. It was the fourth time in a week that had happened.

  "I know what you're thinking... maybe it's nothing. Maybe I'm... overreacting." Liara sighed, loosening her grip on her cloak. "Just Liara being weird about her dreams again. But there's something here. I can't just ignore this. You understand that, right?"

  Timur was silent for a beat as he pushed a log further into the fire. He wasn't one to put such importance in dreams, but it was clear enough to him that this whole ordeal was eating away at Liara. He could tell her it was just a dream; that she was safe, that she could ignore it, that she had to let it go or she'd keep having it; keep waking up in a cold sweat, screaming...

  Instead, he sighed. "Okay," he said. "Where are we headed?"
