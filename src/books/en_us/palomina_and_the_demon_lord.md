## Chaptre I: Night


<style class=".front">In</style> Time Immemorial, the God of the Sun MYSTERIA fought the DÆMON LORD MELIMONAS and sealed It away, deep below the Earth.

For a long time, Humanity prospered under MYSTERIA's Light, building great civilizations and filling many libraries with the tales of their exploits.

But in their endless search for knowledge, they discovered the prison of the DÆMON LORD, and loosed It upon the unsuspecting Earth once more.

Wasting no time, MELIMONAS struck out against MYSTERIA and, catching Them unawares, drove Them from the Sky. The DÆMON, victorious, took the Earth as Its trophy, swallowing the entire world and blackening the Sky with an endless Night.

Generations passed, and Humanity dwindled. The forces of MELIMONAS were relentless in their campaign against all that was blessed with MYSTERIA's Light. Cities fell. Libraries burned. Too much, lost to the Fires of MELIMONAS.
<br>

## Chaptre II: Knight


<style class=".front">B</style>ut all was not lost.

When Humanity was at its lowest, a Hero was chosen by MYSTERIA Themself. She was a Knight, and her name was PALOMINA.

MYSTERIA appeared before PALOMINA, diminished, but still brilliant in Their Glory. And MYSTERIA spake unto PALOMINA,
"Harken well, Child, for Humanity is not yet lost. Thou art their Final Hope."

And MYSTERIA bestowed upon PALOMINA a Gift, a Spear infused with Their own Light.

And MYSTERIA instructed, "Thou must take Mine Light to the highest point and loose it upon the darkened Sky.

"Should its aim be true, and its bearer pure of heart, it shall strike down the DÆMON LORD and drive It from this place."

Thus spake MYSTERIA.
<br>

## Chaptre III: Merchant


<style class=".front">A</style>nd so PALOMINA set out towards the tallest peak, steadfast in her duty.

But MELIMONAS sensed MYSTERIA's Light and set upon PALOMINA with Its forces. Wave after wave of enemies drained PALOMINA of her strength. And just when PALOMINA felt she could continue no longer, she met the Merchant.

But this was no ordinary Merchant. It was none other than the DÆMON LORD Itself, disguised as a mere traveler.

"Ho, Knight," called MELIMONAS to the beleaguered PALOMINA, "you seem troubled, friend. Perhaps I may provide something to ease your passage?"

And MELIMONAS presented her with a second Spear, infused with hidden Dark Intent.

"This shall aid your quest, of that, I am certain!"

Thus spake MELIMONAS.

PALOMINA, being none the wiser, accepted the gift, and bade the Merchant farewell. The forces of MELIMONAS thinned as PALOMINA's travel continued, and though she thought it because of her newfound strength, it was simply that MELIMONAS knew It had already won.

Upon reaching the highest peak, PALOMINA called out, "MELIMONAS!

"I have beaten back Your forces! I have reached this, the highest peak in all the land, and I shall do battle unto You!"

Thus spake PALOMINA.
<br>

## Chaptre IV: Deception


<style class=".front">M</style>ELIMONAS simply laughed.

"Thou seek'st to do battle unto Me? Fine then, Knight. Level thy Spear at Me, and We shall see who is victorious."

Thus spake MELIMONAS.

But when PALOMINA took MYSTERIA's Spear, and heft it above her head to loose it, it shattered as glass in her hands.

For MELIMONAS was clever. It knew MYSTERIA would not let any of Its dark forces wield the Spear. So little by little, bit by bit, its tainted Spear had corrupted PALOMINA, making her appear as one of the DÆMON LORD's own.

MELIMONAS erupted into raucous laughter as the mortified PALOMINA scrambled to pick up the scattered Shards. "See now how thou hold'st no power over Me! But thou hast another Spear, dost thou not? Perhaps it shall be more effective!"

Thus spake MELIMONAS.

PALOMINA, out of options, and not knowing the Dark Spear's blighted provenance, heft the Dark Spear above her head. But when she tried to loose the Spear, it held onto her with tendrils of oozing Darkness and pulled her high into the Sky.
<br>

## Chaptre V: Loss


<style class=".front">"F</style>oolish little insect!" exclaimed the DÆMON LORD, "Thou didst think to best Me?

"Now thou art Mine, suspended in Mine Night! Now thou must watch as thy precious Humanity burns, along with the rest of MYSTERIA's wretched Light!"

Thus spake MELIMONAS.

And MELIMONAS besieged PALOMINA's mind with horrors. Towns abandoned. Children orphaned. Plague rampant. Famine ascendant. Death omnipresent.

The fall of Humanity itself.

PALOMINA struggled against the tide of omens as they threatened to pull her under, deep into a sea of her own despair.
<br>

## Chaptre VI: Light


<style class=".front">"H</style>arken!" The voice of MYSTERIA whispered from the Shards of Light, "Fear not, Child, for Humanity is not yet lost.

"Thou art their final hope, and thou must take what remains of Mine Light, and loose it upon the Sky. It shall strike the DÆMON LORD as a thousand piercing bolts, and We shall drive It from this place."

Thus spake MYSTERIA.

Slowly, PALOMINA understood. She needed not a Spear, but the Light, and the DÆMON LORD was much closer than before.
<br>

## Chaptre VII: Resolve


<style class=".front">"D</style>ÆMON!" PALOMINA exclaimed, her resolve returning. "If You seek Our destruction, then I must not waver!"

Thus spake PALOMINA.

And with a mighty throw, she loosed the thousands of Shards from her grasp.

MELIMONAS screamed, as MYSTERIA's searing Light tore through Its Night, dotting its surface with a thousand-thousand bright, shimmering stars. As MYSTERIA's Light poured through, PALOMINA brandished the DÆMON LORD's own Dark Spear, the Shadow was rent from the Spear by the Glory of MYSTERIA's Light.

PALOMINA took aim at the DÆMON LORD, and once again loosed herself upon It, tearing a shimmering hole straight through MELIMONAS and Its Night. It was large enough to dwarf all others but the Sun itself.

But even cleansed the Dark Spear refused to release PALOMINA's hand. And thus did it carry PALOMINA far beyond the Sky, and deep into the Night she pierced.

PALOMINA would never again return to the Earth, but she left behind the wound rent through the DÆMON's Night; a brilliant Moon to mark her sacrifice.
<br>

## Chaptre Final: Apotheosis


<style class=".front">N</style>ever again was MELIMONAS' Night so Dark that MYSTERIA could not banish it from the Sky.

And as a new dawn rose upon Humanity, they all sang the name of their Hero:

"PALOMINA! Wielder of the Spears of Light and Dark! The Goddess of the Moon!

"May she ever return to us in Glory, and in Light!"

Thus spake Humanity, and thus was PALOMINA ascendant.
