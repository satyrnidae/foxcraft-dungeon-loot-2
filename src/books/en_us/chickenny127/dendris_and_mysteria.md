# Dendris and Mysteria
<vbottom>
---
<br>

`D`drop <style class=".divinity">endris</style> gazed upon their great creation and wept. They were so beloved.

  And yet, Mysteria appeared. A great fire swept across the land. The portals to the land of fire closed, and many settlements were destroyed. The sun brought havoc across the lands. In the deep, <style class=".divinity">Dendris</style> created warriors to fight Mysteria, but they too fled back into the depths and hid in the ground to avoid the scathing heat.

  <style class=".divinity">Dendris</style> leapt into the sky, and hid behind the sun. Mysteria rose in power and spread their will across the land. Most who followed the true path of <style class=".divinity">Dendris</style> were destroyed by Mysteria, and only a few remained.

  Still, <style class=".divinity">Dendris</style> gives us our blessings, and protection from Mysteria and the strength not to rely on the false powers of the sun. The blessings are scattered deep in the dungeons of the world.

  Someday, <style class=".divinity">Dendris</style> will strike into the back of Mysteria, and the deep will reclaim the sky. The fields will burn, the portals of flame will reopen, and the three headed beast will be slain until light rises into the sky. Only then can <style class=".divinity">Dendris</style> strike down the false gods that have taken our beautiful world.

  We shall rise in prestige and power as the world returns to the will of our one true god, and the false gods bow in reverence to the mighty <style class=".divinity">Dendris</style>.
