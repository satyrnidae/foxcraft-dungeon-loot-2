# The Iron and the Giant

<vbottom>
---
<br>

`S`drop calito was a young If, wandering through the fields of flowers near their sunny town. After the harvest had finished, seeing the far side of the mountain was finally a priority. None from the town went near the location, so Scalito needed to see it.

  Scalito saw some movement among the brush. After a flash of sweat, Scalito calmed, for it was still bright this day, with the sun still lingering in the sky. Scalito stepped through the brush, and spotted an orange shape. A green stem in its back, ridges of bright orange stood out against the grass it grew from.

  Scalito was joyful at finding such a nice thing, and began to speak to it. Scalito told it stories of the village, of the past, of the present, and of hopes and dreams. Scalito talked long, and wondered much why to tell this pumpkin so much.

  Scalito did not stop until a groan and rustling of brush broke the mood. Scalito looked up and saw a dark sky, behind and saw rotting flesh. As Scalito started to run, he tripped over the pumpkin, kicking it down the slope they stood on. The pumpkin rolled down to a small cave, which Scalito followed it into. Scalito sealed the cave shut and curled up. Only a few bits of wood and a pumpkin, there was no choice but to wait for the dawn, to hope the sun would dispel the dangers.

  As Scalito settled in for the long night, the pumpkin rolled over and demanded an apology. Scalito threw a stick and screamed, but seeing that this changed nothing, picked up the stick and continued screaming. The screaming also failing to change anything about the situation, Scalito apologized to the pumpkin. The pumpkin began telling stories of the things it had seen. The pumpkin too had dreams and hopes. It longed to move, to walk on its own, without waiting for the benefit of an If to kick it down a hill for a change of scenery. The pumpkin made a deal with Scalito: In exchange for a body, the pumpkin would remove the monster from outside the cave. Scalito was with no reason to refuse, and so agreed.

  The If spent much time trying to build a body. One of sticks was too flimsy, and cracked under the weight of the pumpkin. One of stone could hold the weight, but was so rigid the pumpkin could not move it. Finally, Scalito build a body of iron, and the pumpkin walked forward. In celebration, Scalito carved a face into the pumpkin, so others could see the joy of the pumpkin.

  Finally, they opened the entrance of the cave, and there was no monster. The sun had come, and the monster was no more in the morning light. Scalito looked to the pumpkin, unsure of what to do. The pumpkin grinned, and walked into the distance, and Scalito walked back to the village and said nothing of his adventure.

  Many years passed, and Scalito grew old. There were many young Ifs who lived in the village, and they had a bad tendency to wander far. Whenever they stayed out too long, they always made it home the next day. Some spoke of a loud noise in the bushes, the sounds of fighting.

  Some spoke of a flash of orange and a call to run. Some would not speak at all, but were not afraid. And Scalito knew. And smiled.
