`P`drop otions, once understood, are basic tools that have a variety of uses.  Each potion has its own effects that all can be altered using various reagents.  This book is designed for people with no knowledge of potions, to assist them in learning the basics.  As such, it will go into more depth than most readers will need.

  We apologize for the inconvenience, but understand that others have less knowledge of such things and will appreciate the help.  So without further ado, let's view the main aspects of potion brewing:  [Brewing Stands](#brewing-stands), [Reagents](#reagents), and [Modifiers](#modifiers).  Each of these will be discussed in detail.  For those of you who have brewed before, feel free to skip to the Reagents section, or skip to the [Quick Guide](#quick-guide) for recipes.

## Brewing Stands
  [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/brewing_stand"}} Brewing Stands](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing_Stand) are the most important part of making potions.  To be made, they require a row of [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/cobblestone"}} Cobblestone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cobblestone) across the bottom of the [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/crafting_table_top"}} Crafting Table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafting_Table), with a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/blaze_rod"}} Blaze Rod](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_Rod) in the center slot.

  Getting your Blaze Rod will be the most difficult part of making your Brewing Stand, as Blaze Rods can only be found by killing a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvZGVlMjNkYzdhMTBjNmE4N2VmOTM3NDU0YzBlOTRlZDQyYzIzYWE2NDFhOTFlZDg0NzBhMzA0MmQwNWM1MmM1MiJ9fX0="}]}}} Blaze](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze), found within a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|Nether Fortress](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress), obviously within [Link to Minecraft Wiki|The Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether).  If you do not have access to the fortress yourself, some trading of materials could be your best option to get one.  No matter what, you'll need your Blaze Rod to make a Brewing Stand.  Also, be careful you are receiving {{"sprite":"item/blaze_rod"}} Blaze Rods and not [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/blaze_powder"}} Blaze Powder](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_Powder), which is different.

  Once you have crafted your Brewing Stand, you are ready to use it.  To use it, right-click on the bottom portion of the stand.  This will bring up the [Link to Minecraft Wiki|Brewing Screen](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing_Stand#Usage).  You should see three slots across the bottom and one slot at the top.  In the bottom slots you will place your [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Water Bottles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Water_Bottle), which you can obtain by crafting [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/glass_bottle"}} Bottles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glass_Bottle) out of a bowl shape of [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/glass"}} Glass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glass) in a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/crafting_table_top"}} Crafting Table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafting_Table), and then filling them from a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/water_bucket"}} Water Source Block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Water#Source_blocks), or with water from a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/cauldron"}} Cauldron](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cauldron#Water).

  I would recommend a Source Block, as the water in a Cauldron will disappear with use.  A Cauldron does look very nice next to your stand if that is important to you.  Cauldrons are made by filling in a large U-shape in a Crafting Table with 7 [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/iron_ingot"}} Iron Ingots](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Ingot).

  In the top section of the screen, you will place the [Link to Minecraft Wiki|ingredients](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing#Base_ingredients_and_modifiers) you are brewing into potions.  This allows you to brew three identical potions at the same time, using only a single ingredient.  And with that, we move into our next area of discussion:  [Reagents](#reagents).

## Reagents

  For those of you who skipped ahead, welcome back.

  In this section, we will discuss the main reagents of potions, and where to find them, along with what potions they make (with the exception of [Corrupted](#corrupting-potions) potions, which will be explained in the [Modifiers](#modifiers) section).  There are two types of reagents: [Primary Reagents](#primary-reagents) and [Secondary Reagents](#secondary-reagents).
<br>

### Primary Reagents

  Primary reagents prepare your [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Water Bottles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Water_Bottle) for brewing.  We shall begin with what happens to water individually when each primary reagent is combined, and then move on to true brewing.

  Primary reagents include:
- [{{"sprite":"item/redstone"}} Redstone Dust](#mistakes)
- [{{"sprite":"item/glowstone_dust"}} Glowstone Dust](#mistakes)
- [{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eyes](#potions-of-weakness)
- [{{"sprite":"item/gunpowder"}} Gunpowder](#splash-water-bottles)
- [{{"sprite":"item/nether_wart"}} Nether Wart](#awkward-potions)
<br>

#### Mistakes

  If it is [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/glowstone_dust"}} Glowstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glowstone_Dust), it makes a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Thick Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Thick_Potion).  If it is anything other than [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/nether_wart"}} Nether Wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Wart), [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/gunpowder"}} Gunpowder](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Gunpowder), or [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eyes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye), it creates a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Mundane Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mundane_Potion).  Thick and Mundane Potions cannot be used in potion crafting.

#### Potions of Weakness
  
  The insertion of a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye) as the first ingredient turns your bottles into [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #484d48`potion Potions of Weakness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Weakness).  In general, Potions of Weakness pale in comparison to the other things you can make, but they can be very cheap if made in this manner.

#### Splash Water Bottles

  The insertion of [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/gunpowder"}} Gunpowder](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Gunpowder) as the first ingredient makes your bottles become [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`splash #385dc6`potion Splash Water Bottles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Water_Bottle#Splash_and_lingering_water_bottles).  These are generally useless, but they can be used to:
- Extinguish up to five Fire blocks
- Deal minimal damage to [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvODk3N2E5NGYwMjQ5OGNhZDBjZmRiNjVjYTdjYjcyZTIzMTExYTkxNGQ4YzY3MGFjY2NjN2E2NWIzNDdkNzc3NiJ9fX0="}]}}} Endermen](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Enderman), [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvOWM0MGZhZDFjMTFkZTllNjQyMmI0MDU0MjZlOWI5NzkwN2YzNWJjZTM0NWUzNzU4NjA0ZDNlN2JlN2RmODg0In19fQ=="}]}}} Striders](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Strider), [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvY2FhM2UxN2VmMWIyOWE0Yjg3ZmE0M2RlZTFkYjEyYzQxZmQzOWFhMzg3ZmExM2FmMmEwNzliNWIzNzhmZGU4YiJ9fX0="}]}}} Snow Golems](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snow_Golem), and [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvZGVlMjNkYzdhMTBjNmE4N2VmOTM3NDU0YzBlOTRlZDQyYzIzYWE2NDFhOTFlZDg0NzBhMzA0MmQwNWM1MmM1MiJ9fX0="}]}}} Blazes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze)
- Prevent [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvNjY3ZTE1ZWFiNzMwNjRiNjY4MGQxZGI5OGJhNDQ1ZWQwOTE0YmEzNWE3OTk5OTdjMGRhMmIwM2ZmYzNhODgyNiJ9fX0="}]}}} Axolotls](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Axolotl) from drying out.

  They can be brewed into [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`lingering #385dc6`potion Lingering Water Bottles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Water_Bottle#Splash_and_lingering_water_bottles) by combining them with [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/dragon_breath"}} Dragon's Breath](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dragon%27s_Breath) (which do nothing on their own), and both can be further combined with the other [Primary Reagents](#primary-reagents) to turn them into [more useful potion bases](#awkward-potions).

#### Awkward Potions

  The insertion of a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/nether_wart"}} Nether Wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Wart) as the first ingredient creates an [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Awkward Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Awkward_Potion), which is the true goal of potion making.  From it, any type of potion can be brewed.  Nether Warts can only be found inside [Link to Minecraft Wiki|Nether Fortresses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress), but can be grown on [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/soul_sand"}} Soul Sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Soul_Sand) in other places.  A Nether Wart farm is a must for all potion-makers.

  Once you have your Awkward Potion, you're ready to begin adding [Secondary Reagents](#secondary-reagents).
<br>

### Secondary Reagents

  Secondary reagents are the items that, when added to your [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Awkward Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Awkward_Potion), actually impart an effect on your potions.  Secondary reagents include:
- [{{"sprite":"item/sugar"}} Sugar](#sugar)
- [{{"sprite":"item/rabbit_foot"}} Rabbit's Foot](#rabbit-s-foot)
- [{{"sprite":"item/glistering_melon_slice"}} Glistering Melons](#glistering-melon-slice)
- [{{"sprite":"item/spider_eye"}} Spider Eyes](#spider-eye)
- [{{"sprite":"item/blaze_powder"}} Blaze Powder](#blaze-powder)
- [{{"sprite":"item/golden_carrot"}} Golden Carrots](#golden-carrot)
- [{{"sprite":"item/ghast_tear"}} Ghast Tears](#ghast-tear)
- [{{"sprite":"item/pufferfish"}} Pufferfish](#pufferfish)
- [{{"sprite":"item/magma_cream"}} Magma Cream](#magma-cream)
- [{{"sprite":"item/turtle_helmet"}} Turtle Shells](#turtle-shell)
- [{{"sprite":"item/phantom_membrane"}} Phantom Membranes](#phantom-membrane)
- [{{"sprite":"item/breeze_rod"}} Breeze Rods](#breeze-rod)
- [{{"sprite":"block/stone"}} Stone](#stone)
- [{{"sprite":"block/cobweb"}} Cobwebs](#cobweb)
- [{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eyes](#fermented-spider-eye)
- [{{"sprite":"block/slime_block"}} Slime Blocks](#slime-block)

#### Sugar

  The first ingredient to discuss will be [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/sugar"}} Sugar](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sugar).  The addition of Sugar as the second ingredient will create a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #33ebff`potion Potion of Swiftness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Swiftness), increasing the user's rate of movement.

#### Rabbit's Foot

  Adding a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/rabbit_foot"}} Rabbit's Foot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rabbit%27s_Foot) as the second ingredient will create a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #fdff84`potion Potion of Leaping](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Leaping), granting [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"mob_effect/jump_boost"}} Jump Boost](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Jump_Boost), which will allow the user to jump up to two and a half times their normal jump height.

#### Glistering Melon Slice

  [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/glistering_melon_slice"}} Glistering Melons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glistering_Melon_Slice) are made by surrounding one [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/melon_slice"}} Melon Slice](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Melon_Slice) with 8 [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/gold_nugget"}} Gold Nuggets](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Gold_Nugget).  When this is used as the second ingredient after the [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/nether_wart"}} Nether Wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Wart), it creates a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #f82423`potion Potion of Healing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Healing), which is immediate.

#### Spider Eye

  The addition of a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/spider_eye"}} Spider Eye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spider_Eye) to the [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Awkward Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Awkward_Potion) will create a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #87a363`potion Potion of Poison](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Poison), which will poison anyone under its effect.

#### Blaze Powder

  [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/blaze_powder"}} Blaze Powder](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_Powder), when added to an [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Awkward Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Awkward_Potion), creates a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #932423`potion Potion of Strength](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Strength), which increases the amount of damage each left-click does to an enemy.

#### Golden Carrot

  You can add a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/golden_carrot"}} Golden Carrot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Golden_Carrot) as a reagent as well.  Golden Carrots are made by surrounding a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/carrot"}} Carrot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/carrot) with [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/gold_nugget"}} Gold Nuggets](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Gold_Nugget), not ingots, in a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/crafting_table_top"}} Crafting Table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafting_Table).  Golden Carrots create [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #1f1fa1`potion Potions of Night Vision](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Night_Vision), which allows the player to see through the night as though it is daytime.

#### Ghast Tear

  A [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/ghast_tear"}} Ghast Tear](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast_Tear) creates a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #cd5cab`potion Potion of Regeneration](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Regeneration), which increases the rate at which your hearts return to you after an attack, regardless of your food bar.

#### Pufferfish

  The addition of a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/pufferfish"}} Pufferfish](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Pufferfish) creates a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #2e5299`potion Potion of Water Breathing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Water_Breathing), allowing you to stay submerged while breathing the very water around you as if it were air.

#### Magma Cream

  When a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/magma_cream"}} Magma Cream](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Magma_Cream) is added to an [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Awkward Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Awkward_Potion) it creates a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #e49a3a`potion Potion of Fire Resistance](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Fire_Resistance).  This allows you to walk through flame unhindered, which is very useful when in [Link to Minecraft Wiki|The Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether).  Magma Cream can be obtained from [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvYjgxNzE4ZDQ5ODQ4NDdhNGFkM2VjMDgxYTRlYmZmZDE4Mzc0MzIzOWFlY2FiNjAzMjIxMzhhNzI2MDk4MTJjMyJ9fX0="}]}}} Magma Cubes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Magma_Cube) in The Nether, or be made by combining a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/blaze_powder"}} Blaze Powder](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_Powder) and a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/slime_ball"}} Slimeball](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slimeball) in the Crafting Table.

#### Turtle Shell

  Adding a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/turtle_helmet"}} Turtle Shell](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Turtle_Shell) (a rare item obtainable only by rearing several baby turtles) will result in a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #918198`potion Potion of the Turtle Master](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_the_Turtle_Master), which buffs and debuffs you with simultaneous [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"mob_effect/resistance"}} Resistance](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Resistance) and [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"mob_effect/slowness"}} Slowness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slowness) effects.

#### Phantom Membrane

  Brewing your potions with [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/phantom_membrane"}} Phantom Membrane](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Phantom_Membrane) results in a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #f3cfb9`potion Potion of Slow Falling](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Slow_Falling), allowing you to float safely down from any height.

#### Breeze Rod

  The addition of a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/breeze_rod"}} Breeze Rod](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Breeze_Rod) results in a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #bdc9ff`potion Potion of Wind Charging](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Wind_Charging).  This grants the [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"mob_effect/wind_charged"}} Wind Charged](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Wind_Charged) effect, causing whoever or whatever is afflicted with it to explode in a burst of wind when killed.  It works best as a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/splash_potion"}} Splash Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Splash_Potion).

#### Stone

  Adding a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/stone"}} Stone Block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Stone) to your potion results in a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #8c9b8c`potion Potion of Infestation](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Infestation).  Similar to the [Potion of Wind Charging](#breeze-rod), it causes whoever or whatever is afflicted with it to release [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvZjI1ZTlmYWUzNzE2NjRkZTFhODAwYzg0ZDAyNTEyNGFiYjhmMTUxMTE4MDdjOGJjMWFiOTEyNmFhY2JkNGY5NSJ9fX0="}]}}} Silverfish](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Silverfish) when they take damage.  It is probably best avoided.

#### Cobweb

  Using a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/cobweb"}} Cobweb](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cobweb) in your potion will grant you a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #78695a`potion Potion of Weaving](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Weaving).  This allows you to more quickly navigate through Cobwebs, at the cost of dropping a few Cobwebs nearby upon death.

#### Fermented Spider Eye

  Using a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye) in your potion at this stage will basically waste it, as the resulting potion is a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #484d48`potion Potion of Weakness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Weakness), which can be more cheaply made during the [Primary Reagent stage](#potions-of-weakness).

#### Slime Block

  Using a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/slime_block"}} Slime Block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slime_Block) will result in a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #99ffa3`potion Potion of Oozing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Oozing), causing the affected party to drop two medium-sized [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvYzA2NDI0ZWM3YTE5NmIxNWY5YWQ1NzMzYTM2YTZkMWYyZTZhMGQ0MmZmY2UxZTE1MDhmOTBmMzEyYWM0Y2FlZCJ9fX0="}]}}} Slimes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Slime) upon death.
<br>

### Reagents in Summary

  These are all the main base potions.  In general, always use [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/nether_wart"}} Nether Wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Wart) as your [first reagent](#primary-reagents), making an [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #385dc6`potion Awkward Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Awkward_Potion).  From there, use any other reagents to make the aforementioned potions.  Now it's time to look at [modifying](#modifiers) potions to make new or better ones.

## Modifiers

  Adding modifiers to potions is done to increase their duration, enhance a potion, corrupt a potion, or change how it is applied.  The reagents for this process include:
- [{{"sprite":"item/redstone"}} Redstone](#potency-and-duration)
- [{{"sprite":"item/glowstone_dust"}} Glowstone Dust](#potency-and-duration)
- [{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eyes](#corrupting-potions)
- [{{"sprite":"item/gunpowder"}} Gunpowder](#splash-potions)
- [{{"sprite":"item/dragon_breath"}} Dragon Breath](#lingering-potions-and-tipped-arrows)

  [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/redstone"}} Redstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_Dust) is added to increase the length of time that a potion affects the player.  [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/glowstone_dust"}} Glowstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glowstone_Dust) is added to a potion to make it more [potent], increasing its effects, but decreasing the length of time it affects the player.  [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eyes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye) change the effect of the potion, causing it to become something entirely new.
<br>

### Potency and Duration

  In this section, I shan't elaborate further on duration versus potency, only because it is self-explanatory.  However, I will state that certain potions cannot have an increased potency, so an increase in duration is most appropriate for them.  Other potions' effects are instant, and thus cannot have their duration increased, so only their potency can be affected.  Potency also decreases the duration of the effect, and the two modifiers cannot be combined at the same time, so that should be factored into your decision.

### Corrupting Potions

  The most complex alterations to potions are made through the use of [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eyes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye).

  Fermented Spider Eyes are created by placing together a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/spider_eye"}} Spider Eye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spider_Eye), [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/sugar"}} Sugar](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sugar), and a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/brown_mushroom"}} Brown Mushroom](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brown_Mushroom) in a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"block/crafting_table_top"}} Crafting Table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafting_Table) in any order.  There are many potions which can only be made by combining a brewed potion with a Fermented Spider Eye.

  Most corrupted potions can be further combined with [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/redstone"}} Redstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_Dust) and [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/glowstone_dust"}} Glowstone Dust](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glowstone_Dust) to improve their [duration or potency](#potency-and-duration).

  The potions you can create via potion corrupting include:
- [`normal #7f8392`potion Potions of Invisibility](#potions-of-invisibility)
- [`normal #8bafe0`potion Potions of Slowness](#potions-of-slowness)
- [`normal #a9656a`potion Potions of Harming](#potions-of-harming)
- [`normal #484d48`potion Potions of Weakness](#potions-of-weakness)
#### Potions of Invisibility

  One type of potion that can only be created through corruption is a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #7f8392`potion Potion of Invisibility](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Invisibility), which makes the player [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"mob_effect/invisibility"}} Invisible](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Invisibility) (with the exception of their armor and any equipped items).  This is made by corrupting a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #1f1fa1`potion Potion of Night Vision](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Night_Vision).  The invisible entity still gives off small, light green swirls, which are more noticeable if the player is standing still.  They also still make noise as they move, giving away their position to a keen-eared observer.

#### Potions of Slowness

  [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #33ebff`potion Potions of Swiftness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Swiftness) can be corrupted to create [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #8bafe0`potion Potions of Slowness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Slowness), which lowers the affected player's speed.

  [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #fdff84`potion Potions of Leaping](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Leaping) also corrupt into Potions of Slowness when a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye) is added to them.  Due to the difference in rarity between [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/sugar"}} Sugar](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sugar) and [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/rabbit_foot"}} Rabbit's Foot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Rabbit%27s_Foot), it is mostly recommended to create Potions of Slowness from Potions of Swiftness.

#### Potions of Harming

  When either a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #f82423`potion Potion of Healing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Healing) or a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #87a363`potion Potion of Poison](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Poison) is corrupted, it creates a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #a9656a`potion Potion of Harming](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Harming).  This potion automatically deals out three hearts of [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"mob_effect/instant_damage"}} Instant Damage](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Instant_Damage), which can be increased by using [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/glowstone_dust"}} Glowstone Dust](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glowstone_Dust) to six hearts.

#### Potions of Weakness

  When a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #cd5cab`potion Potion of Regeneration](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Regeneration) or a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #932423`potion Potion of Strength](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Strength) is corrupted by the [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eye](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye), it creates a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #484d48`potion Potion of Weakness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Weakness), which weakens the attacks of that player.  Once a potion has been corrupted, it cannot be uncorrupted.  This is by far the least efficient way to craft a Potion of Weakness, as one could have easily made that back at the [Primary Reagent](#primary-reagents) stage.
<br>

### Splash Potions

  On a note of importance, a lot of these corrupted potions would be a detriment to the user.  A smart alchemist would not leave them around the house to accidentally drink.  These corrupted potions, and their non-corrupted counterparts, can be changed into [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/splash_potion"}} Splash Potions](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Splash_Potion) to be used against enemy mobs, your enemies, or your friends if they try to short you on a deal.

  To make a potion into a Splash Potion, put the potion into the [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/brewing_stand"}} Brewing Stand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing_Stand), and put a unit of [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/gunpowder"}} Gunpowder](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Gunpowder) into the brewing slot.  This will alter the potions into Splash Potions to be thrown at your target.
  
  An oversimplification would be to consider them [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/snowball"}} Snowballs](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Snowball) with side effects.  The reason I mention this is because some mobs, such as [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvZGUyOGU2NjI5YjZlZDFkYTk0ZDRhODE4NzYxNjEyYzM2ZmIzYTY4MTNjNGI2M2ZiOWZlYTUwNzY0MTVmM2YwYyJ9fX0="}]}}} Spiders](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spider), are [Link to Minecraft Wiki|Immune](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Poison#Immune_mobs) to the effects of a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #87a363`potion Potion of Poison](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Poison).  [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvYTUyMGYxMmM2M2M3OTEyMTg2YzRiZTRlMzBjMzNjNWFjYWVjMGRiMGI2YWJkODM2ZDUxN2Q3NGE2MjI3NWQ0YiJ9fX0="}]}}} Witches](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Witch) have a natural resistance to most harmful potion effects.  The [Link to Minecraft Wiki|Undead](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Undead) are [Link to Minecraft Wiki|Healed](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Instant_Damage#Effect) by [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #a9656a`potion Potions of Harming](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Harming), and [Link to Minecraft Wiki|`normal #f82423`potion Potions of Healing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Potion#Potion_of_Healing) will [Link to Minecraft Wiki|Harm](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Instant_Health#Effect) them!  How this works on a biological level I do not know.

### Lingering Potions and Tipped Arrows

  If one obtains both a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/splash_potion"}} Splash Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Splash_Potion) and a vial of [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/dragon_breath"}} Dragon's Breath](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dragon%27s_Breath), they can brew the noxious substance into the Splash Potion to create a [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/lingering_potion"}} Lingering Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Lingering_Potion).  Lingering Potions apply a potion effect at a lower efficiency across a wide area for an extended period of time.  They are especially useful for getting revenge on those friends I mentioned earlier!  Lingering Potions are also necessary to craft [Link to Minecraft Wiki|Tipped Arrows](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tipped_Arrow), which allow for precise application of a potion effect at a distance.  Applying [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"mob_effect/instant_health"}} Healing](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Instant_Health) or [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"mob_effect/regeneration"}} Regeneration](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Regeneration) to an ally with an arrow is a great way to make and keep friends!

### Modifiers in Summary

  Those are the main modifications you can make to a potion.  Extend the time using [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/redstone"}} Redstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_Dust), increase the potency using [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/glowstone_dust"}} Glowstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glowstone_Dust), corrupt the effects to create a new potion using [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eyes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye), or make them into Splash Potions to throw at your enemy using a unit of [Link to Minecraft Wiki|{{"sprite":"item/gunpowder"}} Gunpowder](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Gunpowder).
