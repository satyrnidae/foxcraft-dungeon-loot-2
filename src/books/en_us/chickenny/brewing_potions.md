# Potions

`P`drop otions, once understood, are a basic tool that can be used for a variety of uses.  Each potion has its own effects, that all can be altered using various components.  This book is designed for use for people with no knowledge of potions, to assist them in learning the basics.  As such, it will go further in-depth than it most likely will need to.

  We apologize for the inconvenience, but understand that others have a lesser knowledge of such things and will appreciate the help.  So without further digression, let's view the main aspects of potion brewing:  [Brewing Stands](#brewing_stands), [Components](#components), and [Alterations](#alterations).  Each of these will be discussed in detail.  For those of you how have brewed before, feel free to skip to the Components section.
<br>

## Brewing Stands
  [{{"sprite":"item/brewing_stand"}} Brewing Stands](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing_Stand) are the most important part of making potions.  To be made, they require a row of [{{"sprite":"block/cobblestone}} Cobblestone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cobblestone) across the bottom of the crafting table, with a [{{"sprite":"item/blaze_rod"}} Blaze Rod](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_Rod) in the center slot.

  Getting your Blaze Rod will be the most difficult part of making your Brewing Stand, as they can only be found by killing a [{{"player":{"properties":[{"name":"textures","value":"eyJ0ZXh0dXJlcyI6eyJTS0lOIjp7InVybCI6Imh0dHA6Ly90ZXh0dXJlcy5taW5lY3JhZnQubmV0L3RleHR1cmUvZGVlMjNkYzdhMTBjNmE4N2VmOTM3NDU0YzBlOTRlZDQyYzIzYWE2NDFhOTFlZDg0NzBhMzA0MmQwNWM1MmM1MiJ9fX0="}]}}} Blaze](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze), found within a [Nether Fortress](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress), obviously within [The Nether](https://minecraft.wiki/w/The_Nether).  If you do not have access to the fortress yourself, some trading of materials would be your best option to get one.  No matter what, you'll need your Blaze Rod to make a Brewing Stand.  Also, be careful you are receiving {{"sprite":"item/blaze_rod"}} Blaze Rods and not [{{"sprite":"item/blaze_powder"}} Blaze Powder](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_Powder), which is different.

  Once you have crafted your Brewing Stand, you are ready to use it.  To use it, right-click on the bottom portion of the stand.  This will bring up the [Brewing screen](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing_Stand#Usage).  You should see three slots across the bottom, and one slot at the top.  In the bottom slots you will place your [{{"sprite":"item/glass_bottle"}} Water Bottles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Water_Bottle), which are made out of a bowl shape of [{{"sprite":"block/glass"}} Glass](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glass) in a [{{"sprite":"block/crafting_table_top"}} Crafting Table](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Crafting_Table).  These [{{"sprite":"item/glass_bottle"}} Bottles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glass_Bottle) then can be filled from a [{{"sprite":"item/water_bucket"}} Water Source Block](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Water#Source_blocks), or with water from a [{{"sprite":"item/cauldron"}} Cauldron](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Cauldron#Water).

  I would recommend a Source Block, as the water in a Cauldron will disappear with use.  A Cauldron does look very nice beside your stand if you want to make it look nice at least.  Cauldrons are made by making a large U-shape out of [Iron](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron), using 7 [{{"sprite":"item/iron_ingot"}} Iron Ingots](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Iron_Ingot).

  In the top section of the screen, you will place the [ingredients](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Brewing#Base_ingredients_and_modifiers) you are brewing into potions.  This allows you to brew three identical potions at the same time, using only a single ingredient.  And with that, we move into our next area of discussion:  [Components](#components).
<br>

## Components

  For those of you who skipped ahead, welcome back.

  In this section, we will discuss the main components of potions, and where to find them, along with what potions they make (with the exception of [{{"sprite":"mob_effect/invisibility"}} Invisibility](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Invisibility) potions which will be explained individually).  The main components used are:
- [{{"sprite":"item/spider_eye"}} Spider Eyes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Spider_Eye)
- [{{"sprite":"item/glistering_melon_slice"}} Glistering Melons](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glistering_Melon_Slice)
- [{{"sprite":"item/sugar"}} Sugar](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sugar)
- [{{"sprite":"item/golden_carrot"}} Golden Carrots](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Golden_Carrot)
- [{{"sprite":"item/magma_cream"}} Magma Cream](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Magma_Cream)
- [{{"sprite":"item/blaze_powder"}} Blaze Powder](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blaze_Powder)
- [{{"sprite":"item/ghast_tear"}} Ghast Tears](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ghast_Tear)
- [{{"sprite":"item/glowstone_dust"}} Glowstone Dust](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Glowstone_Dust)
- [{{"sprite":"item/fermented_spider_eye"}} Fermented Spider Eyes](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Fermented_Spider_Eye)
- [{{"sprite":"item/redstone"}} Redstone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Redstone_Dust)
- [{{"sprite":"item/nether_wart"}} Nether Wart](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Wart)
  
  Each of these does something different in the potion process.

  We shall begin with what happens to water individually when each component is entered, and then into true brewing.  The first ingredient is the most important.  If it is Glowstone, it makes a [{{"sprite":"item/glass_bottle"}} Thick Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Thick_Potion).  If it is anything else other than Nether Wart or Fermented Spider Eyes, it creates a [{{"sprite":"item/glass_bottle"}} Mundane Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Mundane_Potion).  Thick and Mundane Potions can only be used to make potions of [{{"sprite":"mob_effect/weakness"}} Weakness](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Weakness), by adding a Fermented Spider Eye as the second ingredient.  In general, potions of Weakness pale in comparison to the other things you can make.  The insertion of a Fermented Spider Eye as the first ingredient makes your [{{"sprite":"item/glass_bottle"}} Water Bottles](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Water_Bottle) become potions of Weakness.

  The insertion of a Nether Wart as the first ingredient creates an [{{"sprite":"item/glass_bottle"}} Awkward Potion](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Awkward_Potion), which is the true goal of potion making.  From it, any type of potion can be brewed.  Nether Warts can only be found inside of [Nether Fortresses](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Nether_Fortress), but can be grown on [{{"sprite":"block/soul_sand"}} Soul Sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Soul_Sand) in other places.  A Nether Wart farm is a must for all potion-makers.  Once you have your Awkward Potion, you're ready to begin.

  The first ingredient to discuss will be a [{{"sprite":"item/golden_carrot}} Golden Carrot](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Golden_Carrot).  These are made by surrounding a carrot with gold nuggets, not ingots, in a crafting table.  Golden carrots create potions of night vision, which allows the player to see through the night as though it is daytime.  If used during the day, it gives the sky a red tinge.
  The addition of sugar as the second ingredient will create a potion of speed, increasing the users speed to twice its usual rate of movement.
  Glistering melons are made by combining one melon slice with a gold nugget.  When this is used as the second ingredient after the nether wart, it creates a potion of healing, which is immediate.
  The addition of a spider eye to the awkward potion will create a potion of poison, which will poison anyone under its effect.
Blaze powder, when added to an awkward potion, creates a potion of strength, which increases the
<br>
amount of damage each left-click does to an enemy.  A ghast tear creates a potion of regeneration, which increases the rate at which your hearts return to you after an attack, regardless of your food bar.
When a magma cream is added to an awkward potion
<br>
it creates a potion of fire resistance.  This allows you to walk through flame unhindered, which is evry useful when in the nether.  magma cream can be gotten from magma cubes in the nether, or be made by combining a blaze powder and a slimeball in the
<br>
crafting table.  These are all of the main base potions.  In general, always use netherwart as your first component, making an awkward potion, from there, use any other components to make the afforementioned potions.  These potions can then be altered
<br>
using other components.  So to keep moving along, its time to look at altering potions to make new or better ones.

## Alterations

Altering potions is done to enhance, increase duration, or corrupt a potion.  Redstone is added to increase the length of time that a potion affects the player.  Glowstone is added to a potion to make it more potent, increas-
<br>
ing its effects, but decreasing the length of time it affects the player. Fermented spider eyes change the effect of the potion entirely, causing it to become something new entirely.  In this section, i shall not elaborate further into length versus pot-
<br>
ency, only because it is self explainitory.  I will state, however, that the effects of fire resistance potions, night vision potions, and invisibility potions cannot have an increased potency, so an increase in duration is most appropriate.  Potions of
<br>
healing and potion of harming, however, can not have their duration increased, so only their potency can be affected. The main alteration to potions is made through the use of fermented spider eyes.
Fermented spider eyes are created by placing together
<br>
a spider eye, sugar, and a brown mushroom in a crafting table in any order.
When added to a potion of invisibility, which makes the player invisible.  Becareful, however, as you can still be hit, and you are not invincible.  You give off small black
<br>
swirls, which are more noticably if the player is standing still.  The invisible player also still makes noise as they move, making them trackable, if not very well.
Potions of speed can be corrupted to create potions of slowness, which lowers the
<br>
affected player's speed.  Potions of fire resistance also create potions of slowness when a fermented spider eye is added to them.  Due to the difference in rarity between sugar and magma cream, it is mostly reccomended to create potions of slowness from
<br>
Potions of swiftness.
When either a potion of healing or a potion of poison is corrupted, it creates a potion of harming. This potion automatically deals out three hearts of damage, which can be increased using glowstone to six hearts.
<br>
When a potion of regeneration or a potion of strength is corrupted by the fermented spider eye, it creates a potion of weakness, which weakens the attacks of that player. Once a potion has been corrupted, it cannot be uncorrupted.
<br>
On a note of importance, a lot of these corrupted potions would be a detriment to the user, which leads to a smart player not having them around the house to accidentally drink.  These potions can be changed into splash potions however, to be used against
<br>
enemy mobs, your enemies, or your friends if they try to short you on a deal. To make a potion into a splash potion, put the potion into the brewing stand, and put a unit of gunpowder into the brewing slot.  This will alter the potions into splash potions
<br>
to be thrown at your target.  An oversimplification would be to consider them snowballs with side effects.  This can also be done with potions of healing.  The reason i mention this is because some mobs, such as zombies and skeletons, are dead, and
<br>
therefore immune to the affects of a potion of poison.  However since they are dead, potions of healing will harm them.  How this works on a biological level i do not know, but potions of healing harm all undead mobs.  Those are the main alterations you a
<br>
potion.  Extend the time using redstone, increace the potency using glowstone, corrupt the effects to create a new potion using fermented spider eyes, or make them into splash potions to throw at your enemy using a unit of gunpowder.

      Quick Guide

This is for quick reference to make most potions, and is not at all indepth, but for use and quick refence by those who have forgotten ingredients for a specific potion.
<br>
Potion of Night Vision

Netherwart, Golden Carrot, Recommended Redstone.
<br>
Potion of Invisibility

Netherwart, Golden Carrot, Fermented Spider Eye, Recommended Redstone.
<br>
Potion of Fire Resistance

Netherwart, Magma Cream, Recommended Redstone.
<br>
Potion of Swiftness

Netherwart, Sugar, Either Redstone or Glowstone.
<br>
Potion of Slowness

Netherwart, Sugar/Magma Cream, Fermented Spider Eye, Recommended Redstone, Gunpowder.
<br>
Potion of Healing

Netherwart, Glistering Melon, Recommended Glowstone, Recommended Some Gunpowder.
<br>
Potion of Poison

Netherwart, Spider Eye, Recommended Redstone, Gunpowder.
<br>
Potion of Harming

Netherwart, Spider Eye/Glistering Melon, Fermented Spider Eye, Recommended Glowstone, Gunpowder.
<br>
Potion of Regeneration

Netherwart, Ghast Tear, Recommended Redstone.
<br>
Potion of Strength

Netherwart, Blaze Powder, Recommended Either Glowstone or Redstone.
<br>
Potion of Weakness

Fermented Spider Eye, Recommended Redstone, Gunpowder
