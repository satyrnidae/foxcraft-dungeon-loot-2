# June 14th, 2XXX

  So... we're fighting them, they're fighting us. Neither of us refuses to cede even an inch. Our greatest military minds hatch a plan: they give up. Not entirely, mind you: they set up a DMZ. A huge wall and an area of land so treacherous neither us nor them can get through it without suffering major casualties. And they sit their <filter as="butts">asses</filter> down and wait. The idea is, eventually the aliens will get so complacent that humanity will be able to just spin back up its war efforts and wipe them out in a couple of generations.

  Couple generations pass, about a hundred years. Humanity has, by and large, not even seen an alien in that time, but *has* been lying in wait, training for the moment that we strike back. Our AIs and databanks keeping the strategic knowledge alive. Right?

  *Eehrt*, WRONG! Turns out the aliens we left our walls to fight are the same <filter as="dang">damn</filter> aliens our forebears fought, down to the individual! And they haven't lost a step. Whatever they endured on the long trip here, combined with three hundred years of fighting us? A million times more effective a teacher than sims and drills could ever be. Our greatest military minds put their heads together and not a one thought "Wait, what if these things don't age like us?" and now WE'RE the complacent ones losing steps in a war that used to be a stalemate.
