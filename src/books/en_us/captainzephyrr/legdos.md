<center style=".title">Legdos L'Tunapos</center>

<center style=".title">Scribdos via L'Scribos Descend'ar L'Tunapos</center>
<br>

`E`drop ndo time lonago l'dez was no ue barra wasten. Thrinas citadeles dotted l'undal ald l'sky was mosit'con precipat. Do dia ue mitos dragon swipa duun ue destroyed tem endo do fell divos. L'tunapos banded despues ue mitos warlos ue las jurnos out ento l'jungas ar'figh l'beast. But l'kratus escapo ar its own demesne. It exactos its revenog via takos l'pos con it ue tranos tem ento its dark serose.
