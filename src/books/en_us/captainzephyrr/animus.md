# ANIMUS OF THE UNDERWORLD

<!-- vertical-align-bottom -->

<center class=".title">by</center>
<center class=".title">Groggus Everus</center>
<!-- page-break -->

<span class="drop">H</span>atred abounds among the great, fiery shelves of the Nether.

  A classic example of this animus lies between the stalwart pigman citizens and the unearthly wraiths that glide above the moldy tracts of netherrack. The rivalry of these races, as detailed by ancient pigman tomes, began thousands of years before the present day, 1400 SU, after the two fought in a vicious war. Neither side could best the other, and, over many, many generations, the hostilities died down. However, small communities of pigmen tend to assault their ancient enemies on sight.

  These vicious battles often last for quite a long time, for the pigmen's more recent affinity for fire renders the wraiths' attacks ineffectual. Meanwhile, the pigmen, whose ancestors taught them to fear the marauding wraiths and their tortures, usually turn tail and run even after summoning the courage for an initial advance. Archers are usually luckier, forcing their adversaries away or even killing them.
