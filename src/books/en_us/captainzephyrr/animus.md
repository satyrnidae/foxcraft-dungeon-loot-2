# ANIMUS OF THE UNDERWORLD

<vbottom>

<center class=".title">by</center>
<center class=".title">Groggus Everus</center>
<br>

`H`drop atred abounds among the great, fiery shelves of the Nether.

  A classic example of this animus lies between the stalwart pigman citizens and the unearthly wraiths that glide above the moldy tracts of netherrack. The rivalry of these races, as detailed by ancient pigman tomes, began thousands of years before the present day, 1400SU, after the two battled each other in a vicious war. Neither side could best the other, and, over many, many generations, the hostilities died down. However, small communities of pigmen tend to assault their ancient enemies on sight.

  These vicious battles often last for quite a long time, for the more recent affinity of the pigmen with fire renders the wraiths' attacks ineffectual. Meanwhile, the pigmen, whose ancestors taught them to fear the marauding wraiths and their tortures, usually turn tail and run even after summoning the courage for an initial advance. The archers most commonly have more luck, forcing their adversaries away or even killing them.
