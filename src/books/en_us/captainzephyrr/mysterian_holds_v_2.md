# Foreword

The land of Mysteria: divided into varied lands and holdings of quite diverse natures. In this series of documentaries, I will examine each of the domains located within the bounds of this mighty continent. The first region of interest to me is the swamp about the town of Foxtrot. This place had humble beginnings, but gradually evolved into a key holding in Mysteria. Foxtrot started out as little more than a complex bunker in a land largely unexplored. The construction of a single house, belonging to Mayor Boredfox, expedited the expansion of the town. Soon, there were fields for crops, pumpkins, melons, and sugarcane along with a storage house. Eventually, many more people ask for and summarily received houses within the town; there are eight in total, including the storage house, Shriner's Insurance Company, and Foxtrot Station.
<br>
# The Mayor's House

The bunker of the mayor's house (the most elegant place in all of Foxtrot) runs as a staircase deep into the stone beneath the Northern Ocean. It contains an apothecary, a library, and a room featuring a Nether Portal. The bunker also contains a path leading to the tracks extending out from the station. A large tree is known to grow in a gorge in this place.
<br>
# Shriner's Insurance Company

Shriner's Insurance Company is the newest addition to Foxtrot. It was created to be a reliable source for creating and customizing an insurance plan to protect one's supplies from any sort of harm.
<br>
# Afterword

Foxtrot is well connected with the outside world, with tracks leading to the remains of Deinotown and a road extending out to Thalbodir. The swampland beyond the boundaries of Foxtrot to the west are extensive and dangerous, so only the brave or foolish journey within, especially under the veil of darkness.
