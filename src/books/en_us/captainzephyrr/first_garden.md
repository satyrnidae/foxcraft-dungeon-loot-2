This is the First Garden, the most ancient and thus longest hidden. Many ages ago, this land of growth covered a large tract of the jungle floor, but the Great Druids sank it beneath the leaves to protect it from undeserving eyes.

Still, there are many who stumble upon the entrance and descend to become a caretaker of the lost domain. Yet others who have come across this place in ages long past were possessed of twisted hearts and sought to utilize its arcane energy for the furthering of their own goals and not those of the earth. But never has any succeeded for the actions of the Assigned and of the Baying Ones.

It is believed that, should a villainous soul overtake these Guardinals and gain access to the powers of the druids, a cataclysm shall befall all of the land and the Shadow will prevail in its ages-long war with the Light.
