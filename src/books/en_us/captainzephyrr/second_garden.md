This is the second Lost Garden, and thus the second to be hidden away from the world. Once, this garden shone its pearly light across the plains as a beacon to those of goodly humor.

One day, for the security of the domain of earth, the Great Druids decided to hide away the three Gardens, and so they did with the first, visiting the second in later days. To obscure the land from prying eyes, these great practitioners of the ancient magics rose an impenetrable wall of powerful oaks in a circle around it.

Through the singular entrance, the Lake of Enlightenment, many have wandered over the years. Those with good in their hearts were allowed to become caretakers and protect the garden alongside the Baying Ones while those with villainous thoughts have been either turned away by the oaks or defeated by the Guardinals within.

It is believed that, should the latter have ever gained entrance to the garden and defeated its protectors, Shadow would prevail in its everlasting war with the Light.
