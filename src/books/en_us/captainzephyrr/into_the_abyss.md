title: Into the Abyss
author: Captain Zephyrr

*Targalin Forest, Eighteenth Month of the Year of Might's Fall*
---
  They raced all around us, swerving around trees and taking quick potshots at our company, each one reducing one of my companions to nothing more than dust. We knew we only had one chance to escape from the wrath of these vile creatures. And that way was to fall. And to fall a LONG way.

  In the center of the twisted woodland there was a mighty, gaping ravine that opened unto a capacious cavern lined with strands of vines from the jungle above. We needed only to make the leap and no longer would we be in any peril. That was the plan. But the creatures were intelligent, and they cut us off just before the canyon, raising their pikes to meet our charge.

  Pistols flew from their holsters at our belts and we continued on our trail toward almost certain destruction. Leader fired the first shot and down went the enemy's war-commander, hurtling down into the gorge. At this the enemy roared as one and set upon us with long strides of their powerfully muscled legs, swinging their pikes at the offset and shearing many a soldier in two. Four of the beasts leapt straight up into the trees and swapped out their javelins for their signature rifles, which they utilized to destroy my comrades on either side of me. One clamped its foot over a dear friend's face and flipped its leg over its head, completing the turn and driving the soldier's head into the stone. Particles of blood rained down on the few of us that remained as my fellows were obliterated by a multiplicity of polearm stabs and deft shots from above.

  Finally, none but myself and Leader were still intact, but we were hard-pressed and were sure to fall. We made eye contact and I knew what to do. The Leader must survive. I cupped my hands and he recognized the maneuver, sprinting toward me to place a foot in the bowl created by my hands so I could boost him over the enemy and up to the ravine.

  That was the plan. But Leader turned the tables on me and, just as I was bracing myself, he grasped me by the front of my breastplate and flung me over the creatures as time seemed to slow for me. Energy rifle outbursts raced past my falling form and I felt I could see every particle of Energerium within in my heightened state of awareness. I managed to crane my neck to glance back, just in time to see one of the creatures hoist its energy sword and charge forth to gut my valiant mentor. The Energerium blade blasted through him with a spray of crimson blood.

  I landed prone on the rocky ground just behind the enemy line. They whirled to give chase, but I managed, barely, to roll onto my stomach and avoid the first energy blast. The new enemy leader stormed forth and I grabbed at the ledge and somehow found a hold.

  I launched myself into the abyss.
