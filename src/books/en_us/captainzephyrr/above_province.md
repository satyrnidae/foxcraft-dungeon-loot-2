# I

`L`drop ong ago, the South was overrun by hordes of wicked creatures summoned forth from the deepest bowels of the world.

In order to escape their wrath, the Southmen erected towers - towers that split the very clouds. Once the army of demons arrived in land of the sky splitters, they were assailed from on high. Falling back, they abandoned their cause and receded back into the Hells.
<br>
# II

`H`drop owever, their master was infuriated. He halved the buildings with a single, mighty sweep of his fell hand - all but one, which withstood his anger. The Demon Lord scaled the tower and at the top discovered an alliance between the humans and the Lord of Stone. All the inhabitants had evacuated already when a large tract of land fell from the sky and buried the dark overlord with its sheer weight.
<br>

# III

`T`rapped forever, this demon still exacted his revenge upon the humans, gathering their souls together and imprisoning them with him. It is said that the Lord of Stone still dwells in the Above Province where he keeps the humans company in his old tower, shepherding sheep and guarding against the enemy at all times.
