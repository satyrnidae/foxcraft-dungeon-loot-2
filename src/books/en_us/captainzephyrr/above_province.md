<span class=".title">Above Province</span>

<!-- page-break -->

# I

<span class="drop">L</span>ong ago, the South was overrun by hordes of wicked creatures summoned forth from the deepest bowels of the world.

In order to escape their wrath, the Southmen erected towers—towers that split the very clouds. Once the army of demons arrived in the land of the sky splitters, they were assailed from on high. Falling back, they abandoned their cause and receded into the Hells.
<!-- page-break -->

# II

<span class="drop">H</span>owever, their master was infuriated. He halved the buildings with a single, mighty sweep of his fell hand—all but one, which withstood his anger. The Demon Lord scaled the tower and, at its top, discovered an alliance between the humans and the Lord of Stone. All the inhabitants had already evacuated when a large tract of land fell from the sky and buried the dark overlord with its sheer weight.
<!-- page-break -->

# III

<span class="drop">T</span>rapped forever, this demon still exacted his revenge upon the humans, gathering their souls and imprisoning them with him. It is said that the Lord of Stone still dwells in the Above Province where he keeps the humans company in his old tower, shepherding sheep and guarding against the enemy at all times.
