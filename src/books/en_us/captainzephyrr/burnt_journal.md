Sitting Day,
# Vengir, AGA 1369

  This morn a strange light was cast across the palace grounds. I could see its source clearly from my home in the mansion housing: a rectangle of a wavering purple material that appeared to be quite insubstantial. My fellow court governors and I instantly set out to investigate, but the mayor bade us rest until morning.
<br>

Mysteria's Day,
# Vengir, AGA 1369

  This day I came down with copse's flu and could not journey down to take a closer look at the light. The villagers gathered and the mayor ordered the organization of a party to venture inside. About half an hour after this, but one villager came back through, and he was screaming about rotting, emaciated pig people. I find this completely ridiculous, for none of us have ever seen anything of that like.
<br>

Palomina's Day,
# Vengir, AGA 1369

  The mayor kept everybody on high alert this entire day, but nothing happened. I believe this whole ordeal will have no effect on the village after all.
<br>

Toiling Day,
# Vengir, AGA 1369

  O my gods… protect me! Massive jellyfish, pig that walk on two feet, and roiling balls of fiery slime?? This is too much! I wish, o I do, that I could be back at Great Heights, drinking tea at the firepit…
<br>
