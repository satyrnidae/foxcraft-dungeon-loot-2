<vcenter>
<center>The Adventures of Captain Zephyrr, Super Kiwi, and Mystical Antalope</center>
<vbottom>
<center>As Detailed by Captain Zephyrr</center>
<br>
Today, the three of us set out on our adventure to the west of Thalbodir in a search for a land of mycelia and giant fungi, the fabled Mushroom Islands. In the swamp just beyond the walls of Thalbodir, we lost contact with the forth member of out party, Gen. Admittens, who could not tell east from west. And so our trio journeyed out into the wild lands beyond the borders of the known.
<br>
# DAY ONE:
We headed west from the walled city of Thalbodir in the early morning and waited for a while in the fen for Gen. Admittens, who accidentally ventured east. We made the massive desert by midday and were halfway through when night fell. For a few hours we wandered around and clashed with the monsters that rose from the depths every night, but soon we lay out our beds and slept.
<br>
# DAY TWO:
From the desert we continued west through a strange string of valleys, fighting several dastardly fiends along the way. As soon as we exited the grassy mountain range, we voted on a direction and decided to sojourn to the northwest. After swimming across a lake to another large, forested island, we moved again to the west, passing through the village of Pity's Sake. After hanging around for a short while, the three of us began to move through the swampy forest, where I discovered a short cave and we made camp.
<br>
# DAY THREE:
That morning, disaster struck as we were passing into a taiga. After I destroyed two zombies, an explosive creeper moved into my vision. Mystical Antalope attacked it and it detonated, destroying him and a large section of land. After a short period of frantically squabbling over who got his weapons, Super Kiwi and I ascended a small knoll and decided to stop at midday. My companion stayed put as I scouted ahead and ventured south to take in the sights of a large mountain range. I slept on the peak of one of these.
<br>
# DAY FOUR:
This day, I returned to the hill. Here, I created a tall, hollow cylinder of earth and excavated a rectangular room underneath it, below the ground. Here, I placed my supplies to create a cozy hideaway. I spent the following days here.
<br>
# DAY EIGHT:
I came upon a large shaft at the end of a short cave passage, which I intended to further explore later. Also, I fashioned another entrance to the hideaway, this one with a door. I've been abandoned.
