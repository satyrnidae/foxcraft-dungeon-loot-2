I've built a strange contraption (even for my standards…) out in the ancient swamp near my hometown of Dolomund's Footprint. It involves sending a rigged cart around on a circular track that leads into a lava pool, detonating the transport! This has led me to wonder if it is possible to survive in lava should an accident occur while mining. Then, thinking of my dear horse Tillie, I found myself absorbed by the idea of fashioning the finest armor for her, armor that would protect her from such forms of peril. Obviously not while mining.
