# Day One:

  Pulsing, purple lights woke me from my slumber this day. As I left the library, tightly bundled in a cloak to ward off the cold, I found that others had similarly been jolted awake. We all converged upon a spot on the outer wall, where a strange, wavering sheet of light awaited. It was outlined in black rock and constantly spewed tiny particles of violet dust. The mayor bade us rest until the morn, when we could better judge the situation.
<br>

# Day Two:

  This morning some of the villagers swore that they heard porcine grunting through the portal, which we had discovered in an ancient, moldering book in the library. Not knowing what to expect from the portal, the mayor decided to organize a party to venture through it and determine its meaning and any possible threat to the city. Twenty minutes after delving in, one of the villagers came back through. He was covered in scratches with a couple of missing fingers. He screamed for hours about the rotting pig men and their siege machine. With this knowledge, I, as the head librarian, continued my search in the many tomes within the Athenæum.
<br>

# Day Three:

  This morning, one of the pig men described by the terrified villager came through the portal. It looked around, its golden sword out before it, then returned to its home in Hell. I do not know if this story is true, since it seems to come only from the drunken rabble of the bars, but if it is, I know that we haven't much time. Also I discovered information pertaining to the siege machine. The pig men had built a large, six-legged construction that they could use to create a portal from Hell in its 'maw.' This is bad news for the kingdom.
<br>

# Day Four:

  We have been assaulted. I was woken today by the sound of explosions, and when I wrapped myself in my cloak and hurried outside, there was a massive white jellyfish menacing the town. Fireballs spewed from its mouth as pigmen stormed through the grounds, razing houses, their integrity weakened by the fires, to the soil. We have hidden in the library, for I am certain the beasts care nothing for books. If they come anywhere near the Athenæum, the remaining villagers and I will lock ourselves in the back room. Hopefully, that will not come to pass!
