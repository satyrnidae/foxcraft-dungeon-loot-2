# Warhogs

<vbottom>

<center class=".title">by</center>
<center class=".title">Groggus Everus</center>
<br>

`T`drop he moldy tracts of the Nether are home to a vast myriad of powerful and objectively evil forces.

  These include the destructive blaze, the deadly ghast, and the wicked wraith. However, likely the most terrifying of all is the slaughterous warhog.

  These immensely strong demons were once the wildest of the pigmen who fought in the great wars between their race and the hated wraiths as berserkers, unstoppable juggernauts employed by warlords to break apart large enemy forces.

  Despite their deserved reputation as unbeatable brutes, the warhogs were captured by their crafty adversaries in the later years of the hostilities. In the death-fortresses of the wraiths, the berserkers were subjected to horrific tortures that resulted in the utter and irreparable ravaging of their minds. When the armistice was called and the warhogs were released, the pigmen could no longer control them, and they began their perpetual rampage.

  Very few creatures can effectively stand against the warhogs, so their musty and unkempt lairs are often barren, devoid of most other forms of life. I am lucky that, during my many adventures through the Nether, I have never laid eyes on one of these fallen berserkers, for if I had, I have no doubts that this guide would never have been published.

  Happy travels, and remember to beware the warhogs!
