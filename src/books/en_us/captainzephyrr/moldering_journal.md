<center>The Journal of Quentis Vivicci</center>

The mighty keep has finally been completed, despite the many complications that arose during the construction. Ten days in, a wave of big, bloated spiders swept in from the eastern forest and demolished all of our hard work. We forced them back and killed off most of them, but only after a long and tiring battle, during which the head architect was dragged off into the night. However, we resumed our work and rebuilt the fortress from the ground. On the sixtieth day of toil, a large portion of the ground in the basin fell away, revealing a mighty cavern complex and forcing us to rework the foundations on the western side. The men have named this area the Undercroft, after the old catacombs beneath many of the monasteries in the ancient snowy kingdom of Vel Darigon where our lord of the hilltop fortress finds his origins. Recently, there have been strange shrieking and squealing cries from deep in the Undercroft, and today, on the ninety-fifth day of construction, we are to descend beneath and locate the source.
