*To the office of Dr. Felstaff Braun, Department of Archaeology, Tynesdale Academy.*

Doctor Braun,

I am Milonia Melisova, a researcher with the Biological Institute of the State of Thalbodir.  We haven't had the opportunity to meet, but I have heard great things from my colleagues who have.  Your work on the excavation of the lost city of Lai'Tanal was recounted to me by a very excitable graduate researcher (who, she told me, had audited your class on more than one occasion at Tynesdale Academy) shortly after I uncovered this find.  We were setting up camp next to a mouldering shipwreck in the field (studying the migration habits of the Nautilus, hoping to find their spawning habitats... but I can expound upon that later should you wish; I don't want to bore you with the details) and I thought it might not be such a bad idea to do a little poking around.

I have already been advised that yes, in fact, it is a bad idea to disturb a potential archaeological site without proper procedure to catalogue your findings and preserve their context, but that regret is tempered somewhat by the fact that the entire affair was washed away in a severe tempest shortly after we set off... I'm almost grateful for my mishandling of the site!  Almost.

In any case, I should hope that the enclosed leatherbound bible is sufficiently valuable for you to forgive my stumbling transgressions upon the field of Archaeology.  You have already written several treatises on the subject of their civilisation, of course.  And perhaps you'll be happy to know your theories about them being a Sun Cult seem to be accurate... to a certain degree.

Wishing you luck in your future scientific endeavours,

Dr. Milonia Melisova, Biologist, BIST
