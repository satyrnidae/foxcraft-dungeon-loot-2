The screams in the next room grew louder.

Luna resisted the urge to shield her ears; the way that her hands were burning now, she'd probably just injure herself. Besides, the doctors had responded quickly. Frederic would be fine.

Unexpectedly, the screams subsided, replaced by whimpers, and Luna could hear a muttered curse. The door burst open and several technicians and the doctor excitedly rushed through. The head nurse quickly pulled the door shut, slamming the heavy bolt above the handle into place. As he turned around, Luna could see the terror on his face, mingling with more than a hint of sadness. She noticed the expression on all of the other nurses' faces too, and one was clearly breaking down, sobbing silently in his coworker's comforting embrace.

Confused, Luna spoke. "Frederic... he... he's going to be fine, right?" She refused to believe otherwise if she didn't hear it from the nurses themselves.

"I'm sorry, miss," the doctor started, but as she turned to face Luna, she did not continue. Instead, her eyes lingered on Luna's singed acolyte robes. The twinge of discomfort on her face. Her burning, reddened hands...

Fireblood. They were certain of it.

Luna's attending nurse Tamelise had somberly explained her predicament after leading her to the quarantine wards. Frederic had apparently been exposed, and was in the late stage if the illness when she found him writhing in pain in the Arcaneum. The act of bringing him to the hospital, while noble, had allowed the living flame to make its way from Frederic to her. "We're so sorry," Tamelise said, "but there's nothing we can do... Fireblood has no known cure. All we can do is make you comfortable in your final hours."

Making her "comfortable" was apparently limited to a menthol ointment and a morphine drip. Luna could barely think with the painkiller's heavy fog weighing on her consciousness. "...No known cure?" she mumbled. _Would that mean..._

"I'm afraid not," confirmed Nurse Tamelise.

Luna felt numb, but not from the drip. _So that's it then,_ she thought to herself. _Three years of being an Acolyte, and for what?_ Even in her fogged-up state, the irony was not lost on her. _Total control over fire, only to go up in flames. Poetic._ Tears began to stream from her eyes as the grief began to push through the numbness. _I can't save myself... I couldn't save Frederic..._
